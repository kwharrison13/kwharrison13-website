## Key Takeaways

<!-- key-takeaways -->
_A Cosmere-connecting novella retelling the original Mistborn trilogy from Kelsier's vantage in the afterlife. The highlights below capture the metaphysics Sanderson lays out — the Shards (Ruin, Preservation, and the other sixteen fragments of the shattered god Adonalsium), Cognitive Shadows, and the tension between change (Ruin) and stasis (Preservation)._
