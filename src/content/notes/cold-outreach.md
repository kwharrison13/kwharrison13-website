# Cold Outreach

Reaching out cold to people you don't know — often people "significantly smarter than me." Kyle's counterpoint to the common complaint (voiced by [Andrew Wilkinson](/notes/andrew-wilkinson)) that asking to "pick your brain" is an imposition: for Kyle, cold outreach to smarter people has produced life-changing experiences.
