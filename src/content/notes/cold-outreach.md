# Cold Outreach

Reaching out cold to people you don't know — often people "significantly smarter than me." [Kyle Harrison](/notes/kyle-harrison)'s counterpoint to the common complaint (quote-tweeted from [Andrew Wilkinson](/notes/andrew-wilkinson)) that asking to "pick your brain" is "like asking someone you barely know to help you move": for Kyle, cold outreach to smarter people has produced life-changing experiences. He first argued this in an April 2020 thread — see As Someone Who Has Had... (tweet).

The theme became the spine of his essay [You Know Who You Should Talk To?](/essays/you-know-who-you-should-talk-to) (subtitle *The Art of the Intro*); the research doc behind it collects the cold-email clippings and reflections that fed the piece.
