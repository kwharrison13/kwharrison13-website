# Ernest Hemingway

Appears in the wiki through a single anecdote in *[How To Take Smart Notes](/books/how-to-take-smart-notes)* (section 13.6, "The Actual Writing"): asked how often he rewrote his first draft, Hemingway said "It depends. I rewrote the ending of 'A Farewell to Arms,' the last page of it, thirty-nine times before I was satisfied." Pressed on the technical problem that had stumped him, he replied simply, "Getting the words right" (Paris Review, 1956). The book uses it to make the point that the first draft is only ever the first draft. No other Hemingway material appears in the sources.
