# Kapil Gupta

[Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant) recommends "everything by Kapil Gupta, MD" in his book list inside *[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant)*, adding: "Kapil recently became a personal advisor and coach to me, and this comes from a person who doesn't believe in coaches." The specific title flagged is *A Master's Secret Whispers: For those who abhor noise and seek The Truth about life and living*. Naval also names Gupta (alongside [Jed McKenna](/notes/jed-mckenna), the [Vashistha Yoga](/notes/vashistha-yoga), and Schopenhauer) in the bracketed update to his regular rereading rotation.
