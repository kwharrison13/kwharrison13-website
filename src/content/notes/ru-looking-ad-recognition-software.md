# RU Looking - Ad Recognition Software

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- I don't usually deal with inventions requiring mechanical engineers or computer scientists but I'm a fan of big data. Conservatives may fear the loss of privacy but big data collected by Facebook or Google is the truth. And the advertisements you get are becoming more useful. I don't need 100 ads about Viagra, at least not yet. What if every ad board could have a retina scanner, just complicated enough to see if someone was looking at it. If not, it switches. But if it's being looked at, moving on too quickly defeats the effectiveness of the ad.
- http://singularityhub.com/2011/07/12/if-youre-happy-and-you-know-it-facebook-will-detect-it/
