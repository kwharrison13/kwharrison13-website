# Craigslist

Craigslist appears in Kyle's notes as the canonical **lean-winner** and **unbundling** case study. In the Cash—Kingmaker or Killer - Research notes it's the ~50-person company — owned entirely by founder Craig Newmark and CEO Jim Buckmaster — that generated roughly [$1B in annual revenue](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/01/24/craigslist-posts-annual-revenue-of-1-billion-study.html) with almost no capital, filed under the cut "In Spite of Cash" thread alongside [Wikipedia](/notes/wikipedia) as proof that raising money isn't the only path to winning.

It also sits at the center of the classic [Unbundling](/notes/unbundling) narrative that runs through Kyle's [E-Commerce](/notes/e-commerce) and marketplace research: the single, monolithic classifieds board that got peeled apart, category by category, into dedicated vertical marketplaces (jobs, housing, dating, gigs, autos). That same dynamic — general classifieds consolidating and unbundling — is what makes the [eBay](/notes/ebay) → [Adevinta](/notes/adevinta) classifieds sale (2020) part of the same story.
