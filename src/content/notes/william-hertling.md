# William Hertling

Science-fiction novelist whose near-term AI fiction anchors the long read [How To Predict The Future](/notes/how-to-predict-the-future) (by [Brad Feld](/notes/brad-feld)). Feld's piece opens by crediting Hertling as author of the award-winning [Avogadro Corp: The Singularity Is Closer Than It Appears](https://avogadrocorp.com/) and *A.I. Apocalypse* — "near-term science-fiction novels about realistic ways strong AI might emerge."

What draws Kyle to Hertling is method, not just fiction: he "used linear trends… to predict the timing of non-linear events (technology disruptions) by calculating when the underlying hardware would enable a breakthrough" — the [Forecasting](/notes/forecasting) technique the Feld essay lays out ("look at the hardware trend," not the software). Kyle flags the same in his essay [Relationship Liquidity](/essays/relationship-liquidity): a "pretty impressive overview of the process that William Hertling used to predict a number of different technological shifts." The work sits squarely in Kyle's [Historical Futurism](/notes/historical-futurism) cluster.
