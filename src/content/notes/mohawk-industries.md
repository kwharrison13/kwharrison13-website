Flooring behemoth (NYSE: MHK), mostly focused on tile rather than rugs. Cited in [Stewards of Capital](/essays/stewards-of-capital) as a scale benchmark for the rug/flooring market: Mohawk generates ~$11B of revenue yet holds only ~2% global market share — illustrating how fragmented the category is and how much volume [Ernesta](/notes/ernesta) would need to capture for a venture-scale return. Kyle uses it (alongside the Fintel carpets-and-rugs comps DXYN and TILE) to stress-test the math on [John Foley](/notes/john-foley)'s post-[Peloton](/notes/peloton) custom-rug venture: even if the U.S. rug market grows to ~$25B by 2030, a venture-scale outcome implies capturing a 2–8% share of a commoditized, fragmented category where the largest incumbent still holds only ~2% globally.
