# H. L. Mencken

Mencken appears in the wiki only as the source of a single quotation on relative wealth, picked up by Kyle from [Frederik Gieschen](/notes/frederik-gieschen)'s essay "Divorce, Denial, Dissonance Reduction": "Wealth: any income that is at least one hundred dollars more a year than the income of one's wife's sister's husband." In context it sits alongside a cluster on the [Scarcity Mindset](/notes/scarcity-mindset) and comparison-driven unhappiness (April 5th, 2022 — Reading Frederik Gieschen's Writing). The sources say nothing further about Mencken himself.
