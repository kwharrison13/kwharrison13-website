# Don't Rain on Someone's Hustle

The impulse to dismiss or deflate other people's enthusiasm and effort — and the case for not doing it. Kyle arrived at the idea watching a critical thread about [Roam Research](/notes/roam-research) and the #RoamCult's response, which got him thinking about how product communities form and what "raining on other people's hustle" costs.
