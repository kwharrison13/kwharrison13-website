# Neobanks

Neobanks surfaces in Kyle's Contrary Capital notes in two ways:

- **As the canonical example of the taxonomy problem in venture research.** In an April 2022 call, [Francesco Corea](/notes/francesco-corea) of [Balderton Capital](/notes/balderton-capital) used it to illustrate why building a coverage database is so hard: "If you look up Neobanks you won't see the most relevant companies as competitive." Getting sector taxonomy right — categorizing companies so the truly comparable players surface together — is the piece he argued no one has solved, and the crux of a "T-Approach" that starts broad and narrows into deep coverage (April 5th, 2022 — Francesco Corea (Balderton Capital)).
- **As a fintech target space.** In a June 2022 career check-in, [Karthik Suresh](/notes/karthik-suresh) (then at [Blend](/notes/blend)) pointed to financial infrastructure "powering Neobanks" as an area with a lot of remaining room, alongside traditional banking (June 6th, 2022 — Karthik Suresh (Blend)).

## Where it shows up
