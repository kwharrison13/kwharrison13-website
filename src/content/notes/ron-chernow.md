# Ron Chernow

Biographer, listed as the author of [Titan](/notes/titan) in Kyle's anti-library. *Titan* traces [John D. Rockefeller](/notes/rockefeller)'s rise to build Standard Oil, his business tactics, and his later philanthropy. The book was recommended in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack) as part of [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s reading list.
