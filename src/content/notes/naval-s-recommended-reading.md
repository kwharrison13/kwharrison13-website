# Naval's Recommended Reading

The recommendations section of *[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant)*, where [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant)'s book picks are compiled — split into a **Nonfiction** list and a **Philosophy and Spirituality** list, plus his answers on what he's currently rereading. Framed with his line: *"The truth is, I don't read for self-improvement. I read out of curiosity and interest. The best book is the one you'll devour,"* and its companion: *"Read enough, and you become a connoisseur. Then you naturally gravitate more toward theory, concepts, nonfiction."*

Naval leans heavily on **rereading** — *"I find myself rereading as much (or more) as I do reading"* — including [Richard Feynman](/notes/richard-feynman) (rereading the biography *Genius*, plus *Perfectly Reasonable Deviations from the Beaten Track*), and names Jiddu Krishnamurti and Osho as the philosophers he is "pretty much always rereading."
