# Gokul Rajaram

Angel investor and operator. In Kyle's corpus he shows up two ways. First, as a source on org design: his Medium piece [*The one thing CEOs should delay as long as possible*](https://medium.com/@gokulrajaram/the-one-thing-ceos-should-delay-as-long-as-possible-ea28347714b0) argues for de-emphasizing titles in favor of scope and impact — a title philosophy the wiki ties to [Square](/notes/square) as a [Talent Vortex](/notes/talent-vortex) ("battle-hardened at Square," which normalized around "lead" as its one people-manager title). Second, as an active angel and [Contrary](/notes/contrary) LP: he joined the June 2021 sourcing brainstorm with [Eric Tarczynski](/notes/eric-tarczynski) and [Saanya Ojha](/notes/saanya-ojha), walking through his own angel portfolio (Infinitus, into which he'd put $1M; Printify; Secureframe; Pinwheel; a Truebill board seat) as raw material for mapping a pre-emption "universe."
