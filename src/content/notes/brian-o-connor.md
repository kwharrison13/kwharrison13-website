# Brian O'Connor

Brian O'Connor is a co-author (with [A.J. Wasserstein](/notes/a-j-wasserstein) and [Mark Agnew](/notes/mark-agnew)) of the Yale case study "On the Nature of Long-Term Holds" (May 28, 2020) that Kyle annotated in [On the Nature of Long-Term Holds](/notes/on-the-nature-of-long-term-holds) — the case arguing that holding a business for decades beats flipping it, because [Compounding](/notes/compounding) net of the friction of trade (taxes, fees, idle cash, redeployment risk) is what actually builds durable wealth. The wiki has no further biographical detail on O'Connor beyond this co-authorship.
