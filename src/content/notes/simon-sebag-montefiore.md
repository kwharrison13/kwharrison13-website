# Simon Sebag Montefiore

Simon Sebag Montefiore (b. 1965) is a British historian and writer, known for popular histories of Russia and the Romanovs (*Stalin: The Court of the Red Tsar*, *The Romanovs*) and for editing anthologies of historic oratory. He edited [Speeches That Changed The World](/books/speeches-that-changed-the-world), the anthology of history-bending oratory (founding era to the Cold War's end) that Kyle read in 2017 on a recommendation from [Elliot Bay Book Company](/notes/elliot-bay-book-company). That collection sits in the [Great Speeches](/notes/great-speeches) tradition Kyle keeps returning to — [Patrick Henry](/notes/patrick-henry), [John F. Kennedy](/notes/john-f-kennedy)'s moon address, [Václav Havel](/notes/v-clav-havel) — and feeds his interest in the art of moving people with words.
