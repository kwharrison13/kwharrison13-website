# Jack Butcher

The illustrator of *[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant)* — his artwork accompanies [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant)'s ideas throughout the book and is also collected on Navalmanack.com and his own site, [Visualize Value](https://www.visualizevalue.com/). Editor [Eric Jorgenson](/notes/eric-jorgenson)'s acknowledgments describe Butcher reaching out and "offering to lend his enormous talents to creating the illustrations for this book," and call his Visualize Value work "simple genius."

## Where this appears

- [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant) — illustrator; the book's back matter points readers to his further illustrations of Naval's ideas on Navalmanack.com and his work at VisualizeValue.com.
