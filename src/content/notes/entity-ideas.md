# Entity Ideas

> 💡 **Kyle's business-idea notebook** — the running index of ventures, products, services, and market trends he has jotted down over the years, "[business ideas] that have come up randomly or from friends." Each linked idea below is captured at its **ideation phase**; the discrete business ideas live as `stage: ideation` pages under `companies/` (marked with the 💡 callout), and the broader market themes live as concept pages under **Trends**.

**Related:** [Build a Fund](/notes/build-a-fund) · [Y Combinator — Requests for Startups](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs/)

`Entity Ideas` is also one of Kyle's capture tags in his reading marginalia — he drops it on a passage when it sparks a potential company or business model. In [Our Towns](/books/our-towns), for instance, he tags the [Maker Movement](/notes/maker-movement) passages (makerspaces "franchised like gyms," low-volume "microfactory" manufacturing) as latent ventures.

## Product

_Product ideas jotted directly on the page (no separate pages). Sketches saved locally under `attachments/entity-ideas/`._

- A chair you can lay on your stomach to read a book — it props you up without putting too much pressure on your shoulders
- A device you wear on your arm while you work out: it plays music, then interrupts to tell you what to do in your workout. It monitors your movement so it knows when you do pushups and can count for you; when it tells you to take a break, it puts a timer on.
- Solar-powered cell phone case (Kickstarter) — https://www.cnet.com/news/are-mobile-solar-chargers-worth-it/
- A legal pad you can take off and flip at the top to write on the back as easily as the front — two magnet pegs through the top binding hold the paper together; the top strip of the back is also magnetic, so you can pull off the back (still attached at the very top), flip it, and snap it into the top of the other side, making it a whole new notebook.
- **Baby Stroller Innovations** — a baby stroller combined with a robot Roomba that moves the baby around automatically while also vacuuming. Sketches: [🖼️ 1](../attachments/entity-ideas/entity-ideas-1.png) · [🖼️ 2](../attachments/entity-ideas/entity-ideas-2.png)
- A car seat with extendable handles and heely-type wheels (like a suitcase) that makes it easier to carry from a short point A to point B without getting out the whole car seat

## Service

_Service ideas jotted directly on the page (no separate pages)._

- **Healthy Lifestyle** — a healthy drive-thru specifically catered to people who want to eat healthy but are busy. Sketch: [🖼️ image](../attachments/entity-ideas/entity-ideas-3.png)
- Parking-lot marketplace where colleges can rent parking spots to students day-by-day — students pick which days they'll be on campus and only rent spots on certain days or parts of the day (Kickstart PortCo?)
- **What If** — a research institute based on hypothetical situations, expanded from the principles in the *What If?* book
- An Alibaba-type website but rather than buying products, you're finding manufacturers in China — the "connections to China" that would come up every time a product pitch needed manufacturing
- Restaurant software that lets waiters track things about each table (how long since they checked drinks, got bread, etc.), integrating with a kitchen-side tracker of how long each meal has left. Feature: a pre-tip offer — at the start of the meal you answer "if you got perfect service, how much would you tip?" and it ratchets down automatically as the service score drops.
- A website that finds all the clothes in movies and lets you buy them ([filmgarb.com](http://filmgarb.com/))
- A website that helps you compile playlists or find songs from different movies/shows ([what-song.com](http://what-song.com/))
- A price-derivative operation in the developing world to limit the risk of price changes — farmers can insure against price fluctuations for fertilizer, seeds, etc.
- A weather balloon that goes up, collects condensation, and drips the water back down to supplement an irrigation system
- A rewards system that incentivizes people to go to the actual movie theatre regardless of which chain — "grow the pie" as theatre business declines with streaming (people pick by location/showtime, not Regal vs. Cinemark)
- A geo-lookup app where you enable what you're looking for (food, shopping, fun) and places near you (you set the radius) push-notify deals/specials they're running
- Clothing mending by mail: get on the website, print a shipping label, send off the clothes, and they get repaired in a set amount of time
- "What would it take to get a restaurant to give me a cheaper meal?" — a platform that lets people trade goods for services; digitizing the bartering community
- **Retail for rent** — Airbnb for retail space with smaller shops; makers find space at similar shops to sell their stuff. Make retail space a real estate market.
- **Reservation maker** — an app that sends someone to not only wait for a table but order your food and let you know when to show up so you don't have to wait
- Make Dane "the Walt Disney of VR" — https://twitter.com/Asteroid_AR
- An app that replicates the experience of a book club — win prizes, share quotes (snap a picture, auto-convert to editable text), challenge friends to read a book in a set time
- An app or website that uses simple games and exercises to teach you about politics and international relations
- A gym where you only pay for the days you **don't** show up — inverting the breakage model so the business *wants* people to come (how would you adjust the model?)
- **Airline excess-seat industry** — after the last boarding call, count the open seats and offer them at a huge discount to a standby group of remote-working "go anywhere" travelers; the airline captures revenue on seats that would otherwise fly empty. Capitalize on wanderlust. (See the fuller [Airline Arbitrage](/notes/airline-arbitrage) idea.)
