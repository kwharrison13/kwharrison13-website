# Molly Graham

**One-line:** *Writer at Lessons (Substack) who proposes a career-decision litmus test as a counterpoint to the "grab any seat on the rocketship" advice.*

## What we know

- Author of "Picking Rocketships Is a Bad Career Strategy," which Hunter Walk linked in his August 2026 roundup. Her litmus test for taking a job: *"If this company struggles or fails, will I still be glad I took the job?"* — evaluating a career move by lessons, people, experience, and reputation effect rather than by financial outcome alone (source: [Why Grab Any Seat on the Rocketship Is a Bad Career Strategy (Hunter Walk Link Roundup)](/notes/why-grab-any-seat-on-the-rocketship-is-a-bad-career-strategy-hunter-walk-link-roundup)).
