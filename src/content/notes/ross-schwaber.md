# Ross Schwaber

**One-line:** *Director of Product at Bleacher Report; customer reference on [mParticle](/notes/mparticle) and the [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) category.*

Schwaber was Director of Product at Bleacher Report (~5 years), overseeing analytics and growth; before that he spent ~7 years at OpenTable, where he launched OpenTable in Europe. He served as a customer reference in [TCV](/notes/tcv)'s fall-2018 [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) diligence, on a call run by [Scott Kirk](/notes/scott-kirk).

His account: Bleacher Report first adopted a CDP to instrument events and get a data pipe (an SDK feeding events into AWS S3), and ran proofs-of-concept with both [mParticle](/notes/mparticle) and [Segment](/notes/segment). His buying criteria were scalability, cost, ease of integration, organizational stability, and strong implementation — and he singled out mParticle's sales engineering as "outstanding." On pricing he preferred mParticle's MAU-based model over Segment's per-event pricing, and noted mParticle did a better job controlling downstream vendor costs (e.g. sending a 1% sample to Leanplum rather than all events). He was "very bullish" on the category, seeing the mid-market ($100M–$500M) as the sweet spot, with differentiation moving toward customer-identity matching across systems and devices. Tools he liked included Leanplum, Branch, Interana, and Braze.
