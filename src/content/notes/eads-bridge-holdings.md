# Eads Bridge Holdings

Mark Valdez's firm — an [LP](/notes/lp)/allocator that thinks about first-time and emerging-manager funds. On a February 9th, 2022 call with Kyle (Contrary Capital era), Valdez walked the LP landscape for a sub-$50M first fund: for many allocators "anything under $50M doesn't really work," fund-of-funds chase "the new buzzy thing" for their own LPs, and endowments guard their slots as existing funds balloon in size. He flagged Horsley Bridge Partners as worth meeting — willing to write smaller checks and a strong signal to others.

Separately, the firm is also listed as [Michael Dempsey](/notes/michael-dempsey)'s affiliation on his essay [Company Building In The Curiosity Phase of AI](/notes/company-building-in-the-curiosity-phase-of-ai).
