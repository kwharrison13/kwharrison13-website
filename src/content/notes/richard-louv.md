# Richard Louv

Richard Louv is the author who coined "nature-deficit disorder." In [Education Nation](/books/education-nation), Louv makes the point that some students labeled with ADHD may in fact have a form of nature-deficit disorder: students who can't pay attention in traditional lecture-bound classrooms "come alive when they encounter animals, plants, fresh air, and hands-on activity." "Nature's Ritalin," in his phrase, has a restorative effect on children's minds and bodies — without the drugs. [Milton Chen](/notes/milton-chen) cites the idea in [Education Nation](/books/education-nation) alongside a broader argument for experiential, hands-on schooling.
