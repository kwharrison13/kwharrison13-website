# Jorge Luis Borges

Jorge Luis Borges was an Argentine author. He surfaces in Kyle's wiki through [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant)'s recommended-reading list in [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant): Naval praises the short-story collection *Ficciones* (also published as *Labyrinths*) as "amazing," and calls Borges "probably the most powerful author I have read who wasn't just outright writing philosophy," noting there was philosophy woven in alongside the sci-fi.
