# Tara Westover

Writer (@tarawestover) cited by [Kyle Harrison](/notes/kyle-harrison) in the Why Do Cities Have So... (tweet) thread (May 2020) on distributed living. In the thread's closing beat, Kyle points to Westover — via her [Atlantic profile on rural America](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2019/12/tara-westover-trump-rural-america/600916/) — for the idea that one of the most important possible outcomes of dispersion is "a re-introduction of one part of America to the other," where "proximity creates empathy of experience, which can lead to understanding if properly nurtured."
