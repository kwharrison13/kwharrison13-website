# Gila Hunts

**One-line:** *Kyle's hunting venture — a lead-gen marketplace matching hunters to outfitters, and the first vehicle in the [Luna Hunting Economy](/notes/luna-hunting-economy) under [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing).*

This is the **light identity node** for the company. The heavy context (GTM, take-rate math, outfitter supply, competitor teardown) lives in the private Gila Hunts — Diligence loading dock (`wiki/diligence-research/`). The reusable **market knowledge** deliberately lives in shared concept pages (see below), not here — so it survives beyond this one company and the lodge / owned-hunts / meat-locker / retreat ventures inherit it.

## What we know

- **Model:** starts as a **[Lead-Gen Marketplace](/notes/lead-gen-marketplace)** for hunt outfitters (source: organization brief).
- **Strategic intent:** built partly to give Kyle **exposure to the hunt market for future projects** — the load-bearing phrase. The company is one probe; the durable asset is the market map (source: organization brief).
- **Region:** anchored in the **[Gila](/notes/gila)** of southwestern New Mexico, near the family land at [Luna, New Mexico](/notes/luna-new-mexico) (source: organization brief).
- **Ladder:** the [Luna Hunting Economy](/notes/luna-hunting-economy) expands from the marketplace into owned hunts, a lodge, a meat locker, and executive retreats — each a future `wiki/companies/` page.
