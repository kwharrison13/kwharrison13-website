# William Sheppard Laney

**One-line:** *Kyle's great-great-grandfather — son of [William Laney Jr.](/notes/william-laney-jr) and [Harriet Ellen Fuller](/notes/harriet-ellen-fuller); patented adjoining land at [Luna](/notes/luna-new-mexico) in 1916, consolidating the family's Section 5 holding.*

## What we know

- **Lived 1877–1958** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Son of [William Laney Jr.](/notes/william-laney-jr) (1848–1922) and [Harriet Ellen Fuller](/notes/harriet-ellen-fuller) (1852–1932)** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [Sara Edna Adair](/notes/sara-edna-adair) (1881–1918)** — daughter of Samuel Newton Adair (1839–1924) and Helen Genett Brown (1845–1933) (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Patented ~61.8 acres** at Luna — homestead patent no. 542578, issued **August 15, 1916** (Lots 4 & 5 in Section 5, T6S R20W, the *same* section as his father's lots, + Lot 1 in Section 6). The family consolidating the section across a 24-year span; because the tracts follow the San Francisco River bottom they are surveyed as irregular government lots (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- Father of **[Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney)** (1914–2004), the next in Kyle's direct line.
