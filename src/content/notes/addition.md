Venture firm founded by [Lee Fixel](/notes/lee-fixel) (formerly [Tiger Global](/notes/tiger-global-management)). Led the $25M Series A (at a ~$100M pre-launch valuation) in [John Foley](/notes/john-foley)'s [Ernesta](/notes/ernesta) alongside True Ventures — both early investors in [Peloton](/notes/peloton) as well. In [Stewards of Capital](/essays/stewards-of-capital), Kyle describes Addition as a capable, thoughtful firm investing across categories from construction to lending to AI/ML — explicitly NOT a "Tiger 2.0" defined by the markups and capital excess of 2021 (though Fixel "hails from Tiger prior to starting Addition"). He notes having co-invested with them and seen that thoughtfulness firsthand, expressing confidence that "the folks at Addition weren't flying by the seat of their pants."

## Where this appears

- [Stewards of Capital](/essays/stewards-of-capital) / Stewards of Capital - Research — the counterexample to the "Tiger 2.0" archetype; backed [Ernesta](/notes/ernesta) alongside True Ventures.
- June 24th, 2021 — Gordon Tsai (Coatue) — named in [Coatue](/notes/coatue)'s taxonomy of private/public crossover firms, alongside [Thoma Bravo](/notes/thoma-bravo), Vista Equity Partners, Dragoneer, and Greenoaks.
