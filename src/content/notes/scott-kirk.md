# Scott Kirk

**One-line:** *VP at [TCV](/notes/tcv); kicked off the firm's [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) category effort in 2018.*

Scott Kirk was a VP at [TCV](/notes/tcv) and one of the leads on the firm's fall-2018 push into the emerging [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) category. On Sept 20, 2018 he kicked off the effort — "We are going to tackle this category a bit together and figure out where we want to push to make a bet" — and set the priority target list: [Redpoint](/notes/redpoint), Lytics, SessionM, [mParticle](/notes/mparticle), [Segment](/notes/segment), Leanplum, Braze. He worked the category alongside [Rikhil Patel](/notes/rikhil-patel) and Kyle, and personally ran the Bleacher Report customer-reference call with [Ross Schwaber](/notes/ross-schwaber).

He shows up again in Kyle's later notes: by 2022, per a [Rikhil Patel](/notes/rikhil-patel) call, Kirk and Patel had mapped what sells into the various functions of the office of the CFO (AR, AP, FP&A, cap-table management, IR) while looking at OneStream. In a gratitude letter, Kyle credits Kirk with setting the direction on that formative mParticle stretch — an early apprenticeship in category-building "before the market has decided what it thinks."
