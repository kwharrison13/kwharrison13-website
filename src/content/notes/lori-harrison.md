# Lori Harrison

[Kyle Harrison](/notes/kyle-harrison)'s mother — writing from Albuquerque, NM. She is the source of the family maxim Kyle records in his [Charlie Munger — The Psychology of Human Misjudgment](/notes/charlie-munger-the-psychology-of-human-misjudgment) notes: "Prepare for the worst, and expect the best to come from it."

She wrote Kyle DearElder letters at the Provo MTC just before he left on his mission, and told him she planned to "find a binder that size and just put them right in there as they come" — foreshadowing the very Book of Life — Mission Binder those letters live in (Letter from Mom (Lori Harrison)). In Kyle's own Christmas email home (Email - Thoughts of Mother (Kyle Harrison, Dec 31 2012)), he names her the most deliberate influence in his life, recalls that she named him after a young man named Kyle she knew in her Family Home Evening group at [BYU](/notes/brigham-young-university), and ties his understanding of the [Atonement](/notes/atonement) and of "Godly Sorrow" to her love and expectations.
