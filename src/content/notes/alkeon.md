# Alkeon

**One-line definition:** *Growth equity / venture firm; co-led [Salt Security](/notes/salt-security)'s $70M Series C alongside [Advent International](/notes/advent-international) (May 2021, $625M post).*

Kyle's contact at the firm is [Anish Aggarwal](/notes/anish-aggarwal). In an October 2022 catch-up, Aggarwal shared portfolio/market reads — [Fiddler Labs](/notes/fiddler-labs) doing well, [Papaya Global](/notes/papaya-global) and similar HR-tech names like [Oyster HR](/notes/oyster-hr) struggling — and elsewhere offered a view on venture markdowns: that funds often comp to a market snapshot rather than accounting for rolling multiples, and model toward a blue-sky case rather than a bear case.

## Where it shows up

- Salt Security Diligence — 2021 — Series C investor (alongside [Advent International](/notes/advent-international), after [Tenaya Capital](/notes/tenaya-capital)'s Series A and [Sequoia](/notes/sequoia)'s Series B); Abhi Arun at Alkeon was on the diligence outreach list.
- October 17th, 2022 — Anish Aggarwal (Alkeon) — catch-up with [Anish Aggarwal](/notes/anish-aggarwal); portfolio reads on [Fiddler Labs](/notes/fiddler-labs), [Papaya Global](/notes/papaya-global), [Oyster HR](/notes/oyster-hr).
