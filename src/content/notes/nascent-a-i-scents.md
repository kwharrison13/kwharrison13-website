> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Literally had a dream about this, started it with Preston and a CMO that I didn't super care for (maybe Regina?)
- https://www.vox.com/the-goods/2018/10/24/18019918/ibm-artificial-intelligence-perfume-symrise-philyra
- Work on a research project to study what parts of the brain get lit up when exposed to which scents, and develop an algorithm to find the perfect ingredients for whatever parts of the brain you want to light up.
- If you find that there are three or four ingredients that light up the parts of the brain associated with focus you can create a candle to study to. Or a perfume to have a date. Or a cleaner to inspire or something.
