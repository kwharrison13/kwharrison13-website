# Patricia Thompson

**One-line:** *Kyle's grandmother (living) — daughter of [Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney) and [Patrick Allen Thompson](/notes/patrick-allen-thompson), married [William Dale Shupe](/notes/william-dale-shupe); part of the 90+-year-old grandparents' generation still holding property at [Luna](/notes/luna-new-mexico).*

## What we know

- **Living** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Daughter of [Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney) (1914–2004) and [Patrick Allen Thompson](/notes/patrick-allen-thompson) (1911–1978)** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [William Dale Shupe](/notes/william-dale-shupe)** (living) — the Laney → Thompson → Shupe line (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- Part of the **90+-year-old grandparents with property at Luna** that Kyle's family gathers around each year (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)).
