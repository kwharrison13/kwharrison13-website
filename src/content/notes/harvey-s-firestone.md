# Harvey S. Firestone

Author of [Men and Rubber](/notes/men-and-rubber) (*Men and Rubber: The Story of Business*, 1926), his own business memoir — a book in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read). The memoir recounts building his tire empire and distills his philosophy of work, organization, and self-reliance. It surfaced on Kyle's list because it appears on [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s recommended-reading list in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack).

## Where this appears

- [Men and Rubber](/notes/men-and-rubber) — listed as the book's author (the memoir is his own); recommended in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack).
