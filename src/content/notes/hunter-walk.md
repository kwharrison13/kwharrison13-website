# Hunter Walk

**One-line:** *Co-founder of early-stage VC firm [Homebrew](/notes/homebrew), and a prolific blogger at hunterwalk.com on venture strategy, career advice, and a weekly link roundup.*

## What we know

- Co-founder of [Homebrew](/notes/homebrew), an early-stage venture firm; on a December 2021 call with Kyle he discussed his personal note-taking and productivity workflow (source: December 2nd, 2021 — Hunter Walk (Homebrew)).
- After 13+ years in early-stage venture, he reversed his own prior view on fund reserves: in "[I’ve Changed My Mind. Early Stage Venture Funds of $100 Million or Less Should Hold Almost No Reserves for Follow-On.](/notes/i-ve-changed-my-mind-early-stage-venture-funds-of-100-million-or-less-should-hold-almost-no-reserves-for-follow-on)" he argues sub-$100M early-stage funds should hold minimal reserves for follow-on/pro rata, since the old assumptions (picking insight, pro rata advantage, pricing discipline, historical data proof, signaling risk) have all broken down as the venture market has changed.
- Writes a recurring link-roundup post (source: "[Why Grab Any Seat on the Rocketship Is a Bad Career Strategy (Hunter Walk Link Roundup)](/notes/why-grab-any-seat-on-the-rocketship-is-a-bad-career-strategy-hunter-walk-link-roundup)") curating career, capitalism, and founder-persona pieces from other writers.
