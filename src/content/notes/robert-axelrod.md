# Robert Axelrod

Author of *[The Evolution of Cooperation](https://amzn.to/2N6avzV)*. In [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant), [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant) recommends the book — alongside [J.D. Williams](/notes/j-d-williams)'s *The Compleat Strategyst* and "playing a lot of games" — as one of the ways to learn [Game Theory](/notes/game-theory), which Naval treats as a foundational discipline for navigating "most of our modern capitalist society."
