# Imposter Syndrome

Named in passing in [How To Take Smart Notes](/books/how-to-take-smart-notes): high achievers who've glimpsed the vast amount of knowledge out there are prone to Imposter Syndrome — feeling not up to the job despite evidence to the contrary — because good students keep raising the bar for themselves by focusing on what they haven't yet mastered (citing Clance and Imes 1978; Brems et al. 1994).

It surfaces again in a personal register: on a June 24, 2021 get-to-know call, [Molly Alter](/notes/molly-alter) of [Index Ventures](/notes/index-ventures) described how starting a career in investing builds "this scar tissue of constantly being insensitive" — early-career self-doubt as an occupational hazard of the job.
