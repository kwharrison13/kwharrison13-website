# Luna Hunting Economy

**One-line:** *The sub-thesis hub for the hunting-venture cluster under [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing) — a business that starts as a lead-gen marketplace and expands into owned hunts, a lodge, a meat locker for hunters, and executive retreats, all built on the family land near the [Gila](/notes/gila).*

This is the **sub-thesis hub** where the reusable market map hangs. The strategic bet (from Kyle's organization brief): the first company is one *vehicle*, but the durable asset is **market knowledge** — so it lives in shared concept pages every venture reuses, not inside any one company page. [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) becomes the first probe that populates the map; the lodge inherits it on day one.

## Two ventures, one map

As of the July 2026 research, the cluster has resolved into **two distinct ventures** that share the reusable market map:

- **A) [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) — the marketplace (software).** A verified, hyper-local, SEO-driven lead-gen marketplace matching hunters to Gila outfitters. Asset-light; the moat is programmatic SEO + owning the booking. Full plan in Gila Hunts — Diligence.
- **B) The Luna retreat / lodge — the place-based venture (land + hospitality).** A single buy-out, off-grid destination retreat near Luna (corporate retreats as the year-round base, seasonal owned-hunts as the premium overlay), on the Lodge-at-Chama model. Master plan in Luna Landing Retreat — Master Reference; the land-and-tag strategy in E-PLUS Land Strategy — Luna.

### The venture ladder

1. **Lead-gen marketplace** — [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) (venture A). Built partly to give Kyle exposure to the hunt market for future projects.
2. **Owned hunts** — hunts on owned/acquired land, tag-gated by [E-PLUS](/notes/e-plus) (venture B).
3. **Lodge** — the retreat/lodge (venture B base engine; corporate + hunting).
4. **Meat locker** — a meat-processing / cold-storage business for hunters.
5. **Executive retreats** — the year-round corporate-buyout core of venture B.

_(Each becomes its own `wiki/companies/` page as it takes shape.)_

**Key strategic finding (E-PLUS):** the money in venture B is **not** in owning land for tags — [E-PLUS](/notes/e-plus) tags in public-land-heavy GMU 15 only yield ~$10K–$50K/yr against millions in land, and quietly assembling land shrinks every Luna neighbor's tag allocation (a political landmine). The engine is **owning the lodge and packaging *other people's* tags** into guided hunts — see E-PLUS Land Strategy — Luna.
