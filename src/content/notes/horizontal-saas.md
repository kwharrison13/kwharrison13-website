# Horizontal SaaS

Software that serves a single function across every industry (CRM, HR, collaboration, etc.) — the counterpart to [Vertical SaaS](/notes/vertical-saas) (industry-specific software). Kyle flagged "Bundling / Unbundling of Horizontal SaaS" as a deep-dive theme on his Roam "Trends" idea-capture page, with the central question of whether horizontal categories get bundled into all-in-one suites or unbundled into best-in-class point solutions ([Bundling](/notes/unbundling) / [Unbundling](/notes/unbundling)).
