# Richard Tedlow

**One-line:** *Harvard Business School professor cited by Andy Grove for a business-failure taxonomy: companies fail either because they leave their customers (the "obvious" change — arbitrarily abandoning a strategy that worked) or because their customers leave them (the "subtle" one).* — [Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive)

## What we know

- "In an analysis of the history of business failures, Harvard Business School Professor Richard Tedlow came to the conclusion that businesses fail either because they leave their customers, i.e., they arbitrarily change a strategy that worked for them in the past (the obvious change), or because their customers leave them (the subtle one)." ([Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive))
