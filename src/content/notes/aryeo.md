# Aryeo

**One-line definition:** *Real-estate media/marketing SaaS founded/operated by [Branick Weix](/notes/branick-weix); in Mar 2022 >5% of all US home sales were listed on its platform, hosting properties ~6.82 days before they hit the MLS or [Zillow](/notes/zillow).*

A [Contrary](/notes/contrary) portfolio company whose monthly updates Kyle read through 2022. Its wedge is a **data/timing moat**: a typical property is created on Aryeo 6.82 days before it appears on the MLS, [Zillow](/notes/zillow), or any real-estate search site, and Aryeo knows exactly who holds the original media copyright (April 5th, 2022 — Aryeo Q1 Update (Branick Weix)). By spring 2022 it was hosting 19,000+ homes in a month — more properties per day than most states process.

Strategy through 2022 tightened around that core: in the February update the team dropped its standalone broker product to refocus on the platform and the exclusive content/data it generates (March 30th, 2022 — Aryeo February Update (Branick Weix)). A deep dive into stats found the lowest subscription tier drove under 10% of revenue but nearly 40% of support tickets — feeding a growing [Customer Support](/notes/customer-support) burden that, by the April update, had Aryeo hiring a dedicated support lead to automate inbound handling (June 15th, 2022 — Aryeo April Update (Branick Weix)).
