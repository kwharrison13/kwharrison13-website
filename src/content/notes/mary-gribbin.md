# Mary Gribbin

Co-author (with [John Gribbin](/notes/john-gribbin)) of [Ice Age](/notes/ice-age), a book in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read). The book recounts the scientific history and personalities behind the discovery of ice ages; it surfaced on Charlie Munger's recommended-reading list in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack).

## Where this appears

- [Ice Age](/notes/ice-age) — listed as a co-author alongside [John Gribbin](/notes/john-gribbin).
- [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack) — the source that put *Ice Age* on Kyle's reading list.
