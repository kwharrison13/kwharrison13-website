# Strategic Dissonance

**One-line definition:** *Grove's term for a company "saying one thing and doing another" while coping with a [Strategic Inflection Point](/notes/strategic-inflection-point) — the divergence between a company's stated strategy and its actual actions.* — [Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive)

## How sources describe it

- "I have seen many companies fall into the same trap of saying one thing and doing another while they are in the midst of coping with a strategic inflection point. I call this divergence between actions and statements strategic dissonance. It is one of the surest indications that a company is struggling with a strategic inflection point." ([Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive))
- Grove says resolution doesn't come as a sudden insight but through experimentation: loosening organizational control, letting people try different techniques, products, and channels — "let chaos reign."

## Where it shows up

- [Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive) — presented as one of the surest diagnostic signs that a company is mid-inflection-point.
