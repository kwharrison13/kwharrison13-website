# Ira Rothberg

Ira Rothberg is Portfolio Manager and Managing Member of Broad Run Investment Management, quoted in the Yale case study [On the Nature of Long-Term Holds](/notes/on-the-nature-of-long-term-holds) on the primacy of [Compounding](/notes/compounding): "The power of compounding is so great that our first job as investors is to avoid anything that might short circuit it." The case (by [A.J. Wasserstein](/notes/a-j-wasserstein), [Mark Agnew](/notes/mark-agnew), and [Brian O'Connor](/notes/brian-o-connor)) uses the line to frame its argument that long-horizon ownership beats flipping a business, because compounding's biggest gains arrive only in the final years of a hold.
