# roon

Writer active on X as @tszzl, known in Kyle's corpus for one aphoristic take on belief and the sociology of intense communities. In Accusations Of Culthood Are Always... (tweet) he argues "accusations of culthood are always layered in jealousy. everyone wishes they believed in anything so strongly" — a line the wiki uses to reframe cult accusations as envy of deep [Conviction](/notes/conviction) rather than pathology, tying it to both [Jealousy](/notes/jealousy) and [Faith](/notes/faith).
