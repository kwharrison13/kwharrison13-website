- ==**Overview of the Space**== (From 2017)
  - We spoke with companies and VCs in the sharing economy ecosystem and our takeaways are as follows: "Companies built on top of home sharing platforms are not differentiated and follow a standard playbook"
    - Manage/service/price short-term rental properties across various platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, HomeAway etc.)
      - BeyondPricing, Wheelhouse → Pricing software for homeowner listings
      - Guesty, Pillow → Property management software (i.e. reporting, scheduling) for homeowners
      - Wanderjaunt, Sonder → End-to-end property management for homeowners
    - Collect valuable data to improve pricing algorithms and identify most valuable short-term rental market opportunities
  - 2) None of these companies are interesting for Coatue, but their efforts speak to size of TAM and value of platforms in the space:
    - "Long-term goal is to leverage data to acquire more properties and monetize at higher levels"
    - "Capture more of the economics via an independent listing platform or owned & operated real estate"
    - "While businesses are solid at scale, the model is slower and more operationally challenging to grow, and doesn't lend itself to venture-like ($10B+) outcomes"
      - The most successful, mature examples of this model are Vacasa and Turnkey, which book vacation rentals through their own sites in addition to third-party platforms in return for a commission of ~30-50%
      - They are one-stop shops, pricing listings, prepping space and servicing guest visits in select markets across U.S
  - We'll continue to monitor trend, but it's clearly more attractive to own the marketplace e.g. Airbnb than companies built on top of these sharing platforms
  - **Wanderjaunt**
    - End to end property management company, building a brand on listing platforms (e.g. SPG for short-term rentals)
    - Team is from Doordash and raised $2M seed from Khosla
    - Launched in Phoenix in November-16 where all non-technical team members are currently located
      - More forgiving real estate market
      - Easier to bootstrap
      - Goal is to prove out business model and product/market/fit before expanding
    - 9 properties in Phoenix, of which 7 are active, 2 more launching by end of August
      - They rent properties from owners and furnish, list, price and manage them
      - Many operational challenges with end to end management of properties, including furnishing, guest services, cleaning etc.
    - Listings are on established platforms - Airbnb, Homeaway, VRBO, Tripadvisor, [Booking.com](http://Booking.com), Expedia
      - Airbnb is by far largest today
      - Ultimately they want to build-out their own listing platform
    - Short-term: Scale operations and collect data, build internal pricing algorithm so they don’t need to rely on BeyondPricing (which they view as superior to Airbnb pricing) and other providers and build-out internal guest management software (e.g. Guesty)
    - Long-term: Data will help them to figure out revenue generation potential of short-term rental properties across different markets enabling them to identify most compelling opportunities and make instantaneous leasing offers for properties ([Opendoor](/notes/opendoor) for rentals)
  - **Turnkey**
    - Tech-enabled vacation rental property manager and listing platform
      - Raised $41M to date from Adams Street Partners, Altos Ventures Series
    - Founders worked at [hotwire.com](http://hotwire.com) , and later at Expedia and HomeAway post-sale
      - Core competency in hospitality revenue management
    - Underwhelmed by current offerings, so launched business to manage listing for homeowners across platforms e.g. Airbnb, Homeaway, VRBO
      - 20% of sales from their own listing platform
      - They handle pricing leveraging internal tools and third-party data sets and manage guest services with their proprietary digital platform, which is also used to coordinate vendors in a market
      - ~28% commission (18% owner, 10% guest) on top of other platform fees vs. market rate of 30-50% for Vacasa
    - Focused on mass affluent market
      - 50 live markets, 2,400 live homes (going to 3K), claims to be 2nd largest in U.S. after Wyndham
      - $85M in gross bookings, $22M in revenue 2017, $40M net revenue projected for 2018
        - Cashflow positive in spring 2018
      - Vision is 100 markets and 150K homes
      - Onefinestay was luxury comp that sold to Accorhotels for $170M in April-16 and was managing 2,600 properties at time exclusive to its platform
    - Launching standalone “Turnkey light” subscriptions so others can leverage their reservation / booking technology and digital lock IP (unique codes for services providers to access property)
      - Richard Barton (founder of Expedia, Glassdoor, Zillow) is board member who has pushed this initiative
  - Vacasa
    - KD spoke with CEO in last few months
      - Same business model as Turnkey
      - Raised $40M from Level Equity and Assurant Growth Investing
    - $25B US market for vacation rentals and very fragmented (Europe is $85B+)
      - Wyndam is market leader with 9,000 homes (<1% of US market)
      - Long tail of managers overseeing 30 or fewer homes
    - Vacasa is top 3 player in market with $100M+ annual revenue
- ==**Airbnb Market**==
  - TL/DS/AS/BK/BS brainstorm on Airbnb, sharing economy trend and implications for hotel food chain
    - Airbnb growing to ~5% of hotel nights in 2017 and other short-term rental players (HomeAway, VRBO) gaining share vs. incumbents
      - Airbnb potential $100B+ company over next few years as it continues to penetrate market and expand TAM with new travel-related initiatives e.g. experiences
    - Ecosystem around Airbnb rapidly evolving with numerous companies catering to short-term rental market through hosting (Vacasa, Wanderjaunt), property management (Pillow, Guesty), listing optimization (Wheelhouse, BeyondPricing) and other services (Clearbanc)
    - Implications for entire hotel food chain as Airbnb grow and OTAs (Priceline, Expedia) continue to aggressively pursue growth in alternative accommodations market
    - Kicking off multiple work streams to update work on sharing economy and potential hotel short
