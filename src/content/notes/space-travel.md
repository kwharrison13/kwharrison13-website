# Space Travel

In [The Magic of Elon Musk](/notes/the-magic-of-elon-musk) ([David Perell](/notes/david-perell)'s 2016 essay), space travel is the organizing mission behind everything [Elon Musk](/notes/elon-musk) does: he judges his own success by asking whether his work helps humanity become a multi-planetary species, and that single question gives his deep commitment and [Focus](/notes/focus) their direction. In the piece's causal chain — "Goals breed commitment. Commitment breeds focus. Focus breeds achievement" — the multi-planetary goal is the goal at the top.

## Where this appears

- [The Magic of Elon Musk](/notes/the-magic-of-elon-musk) — the multi-planetary mission framed as the question that organizes all of Musk's work.
