# Stephen Gordon

Credited by [David Perell](/notes/david-perell) in [When Java Meets Jive](/notes/when-java-meets-jive) as the origin of the phrase "coffeeshopification" — and with predicting that coffee shops would soon replace most retail stores. Perell invokes him in the essay's account of how third-wave coffee ritualized the brewing-and-serving craft and reshaped cities, with coffee shops now catering to a range of audiences (rambunctious teenagers, focused workers, coffee connoisseurs). #[City Building](/notes/city-building)
