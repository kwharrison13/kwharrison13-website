# Zac Gorman

Creator of the *[Rick and Morty](https://amzn.to/2N7427M)* comic book. In [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant), among his media recommendations, [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant) calls *Rick and Morty* "the best show on television" and adds that Gorman's comic "is just as clever as the show."
