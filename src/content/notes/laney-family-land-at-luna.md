# Laney Family Land at Luna

**One-line:** *Federal land patents and present-day parcel disposition for the Laney family homestead at [Luna](/notes/luna-new-mexico), Catron County — two generations patented ~208 acres in and around Section 5, T6S R20W (1892 + 1916); none is held by a Laney today.*

> **Why this is here:** the **land / genealogy** record for [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing). Grounds both the novel's character bibles (dates and lineage) and any land-assembly thinking (what the family once held, where it went). Companion to [Water in Catron County](/notes/water-in-catron-county) and the E-PLUS land strategy.

---

> **Scope:** Federal land patents and present-day parcel disposition for the Laney
> family homestead at Luna, Catron County, New Mexico.
> **Compiled:** July 30, 2026, from public federal and county records (see *Sources & Methods*).
> **Survey system:** New Mexico Principal Meridian (PM code 23); Townships 5–6 South, Range 20 West.

## Summary

Two generations of Laneys patented adjoining tracts in and around **Section 5, T6S R20W**,
directly at the village of **Luna** in western Catron County, along the San Francisco River.

- **William Laney Jr.** (1848–1922) — the first Laney in New Mexico — patented **~146.5 acres** in **1892**.
- **William Sheppard Laney** (1877–1958), his son, patented an adjoining **~61.8 acres** in **1916**.
- Combined family holding at patent: **~208 acres**.
- **Sara Edna Adair** (1881–1918), wife of William Sheppard Laney, holds **no federal patent** in her own name (see below).
- None of the ground is held by a Laney today; it has passed to unrelated owners and family trusts.

Approximate center of the holding: **33.818° N, 108.931° W** — essentially the Luna townsite.

## Family context

Lineage as recorded in the family tree, for reference:

- **William Laney Jr.** (1848–1922) m. **Harriet Ellen Fuller** (1852–1932)
  - → **William Sheppard Laney** (1877–1958) m. **Sara Edna Adair** (1881–1918)
    - Sara Edna Adair was the daughter of **Samuel Newton Adair** (1839–1924) and **Helen Genett Brown** (1845–1933)
    - → **Helen Laney** (1914–2004) m. **Patrick Allen Thompson** (1911–1978)
      - → **Patricia Thompson** (living) m. **William Dale Shupe** (living)

The two patentees below (the father and the son) are the direct Laney ancestors who
established and expanded the family's ground at Luna.

| Legal description | Township / Range | Section | Approx. acres |

The son's Lots 4 and 5 fall within the **same Section 5** as his father's lots — the family
consolidating the section across a 24-year span. Because the tracts follow the San Francisco
River bottom, they are surveyed as irregular **government lots** rather than clean quarter-quarters.

### Sara Edna Adair (1881–1918) — wife of William Sheppard Laney

**No federal land patent exists in her own name.** A full search of every Adair patent in
New Mexico (14 patentees; none named Sara, Sarah, or Edna) and of the Laney rolls (in case of
a married name) returned nothing. Practical interpretation:

- She did not homestead land herself.
- Any land interest she held would have been either a **community-property interest** in her
  husband's patented ground (the patent issued in his name only) or acquired through **later
  county-level deed transactions** (purchase, inheritance, transfer).
- Those intermediate deeds are **not** in the federal patent index — they would be in the
  **Catron County Clerk's** grantor/grantee books in Reserve.

## Sources & methods

1. **Original ownership (patents):** U.S. Bureau of Land Management, General Land Office (GLO)
   Records — `glorecords.blm.gov`. Searched patents by surname within New Mexico; patent
   metadata (accession, document number, issue date, legal description) taken directly from the
   index.
2. **Survey geometry & acreage:** BLM National PLSS CadNSDI cadastral service
   (`gis.blm.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Cadastral/BLM_Natl_PLSS_CadNSDI`), Section and
   "PLSS Intersected" (lot/aliquot) layers. Acreages are **computed** from the CadNSDI polygons
   and are approximate — within a rounding margin of the official patent figures, not exact to
   the certificate.
3. **Present-day ownership:** Catron County Assessor public tax-parcel feature service
   (ArcGIS; `Tax_Parcel_Polygon` layer, ~17,850 parcels), queried by section/township/range.
4. **Genealogy:** family tree provided by the family (names and dates as recorded there).

## Caveats & open questions

- **Federal layer only.** This documents the *first* transfer of land from the government to the
  Laneys. Everything between 1892/1916 and today (sales, inheritance, the passage to current
  owners) lives in the **Catron County Clerk** deed books and was not traced.
- **Pre-1921 records.** Catron County was created in 1921 from Socorro County. Any county-level
  records predating 1921 for this ground would be filed under **Socorro County**.
- **Sara Edna Adair's interest** remains undocumented at the federal level; confirming it would
  require pulling county deeds and probate records.
- **Acreages are approximate** (computed from survey geometry, not transcribed from the patents).
- The **1892 "William Laney"** and **1916 "William S. Laney"** were confirmed as father and son
  (not one person) via the family tree; the 1892 patent is the father's, the 1916 patent the son's.
