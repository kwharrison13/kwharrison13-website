# Rapyd

**One-line definition:** *Global payments network that lets companies integrate local payment methods; product is 100% cloud-based and 100% API-based; a [Noname Security](/notes/noname-security) customer, chosen over [Salt Security](/notes/salt-security) after evaluating others.*

## What we know

- A global payment network "allowing companies to integrate local payment methods." Its product is 100% cloud-based and entirely API-based — the reason API security became a priority for its team. (source: Salt Security Diligence — 2021, Noname Security - Diligence)
- [Nir Rothenberg](/notes/nir-rothenberg) served as Rapyd's CISO (2.5 years as of October 2021), overseeing global IT, cloud, and physical security; previously Head of Security at [NSO Group](/notes/nso-group). He evaluated [Salt Security](/notes/salt-security), [NeoSec](/notes/neosec), and [IMvision](/notes/imvision) and became a [Noname Security](/notes/noname-security) customer on posture-management grounds.
- Rapyd appears as one of [Noname Security](/notes/noname-security)'s named customer logos (per the traction read), alongside [Starbucks](/notes/starbucks), Chipotle, and FIS — "albeit not the most sophisticated technical logos."

## Where it shows up

- Salt Security Diligence — 2021 — [Nir Rothenberg](/notes/nir-rothenberg) (CISO) was a reference-call subject (October 4, 2021); chose Noname over Salt.
- Noname Security - Diligence — same reference call, preserved in the Noname dossier; Rapyd listed among Noname's customer logos.
