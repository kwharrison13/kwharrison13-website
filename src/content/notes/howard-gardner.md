# Howard Gardner

Howard Gardner is the Harvard psychologist who developed the theory of [Multiple Intelligences](/notes/multiple-intelligences). In [Education Nation](/books/education-nation), [Milton Chen](/notes/milton-chen) cites Gardner for documenting the "intrapersonal" and "interpersonal" intelligences among the eight multiple intelligences (which also include the verbal, logical-mathematical, musical, and bodily-kinesthetic), and for reframing their role for twenty-first-century thinking and behavior. Gardner describes the intrapersonal intelligence as the ability to identify and use one's own feelings and motivations — a deep understanding of oneself — and the interpersonal intelligence as the capacity for empathy and working well in groups.
