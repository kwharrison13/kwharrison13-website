# Public Market Signals

A sourcing principle Kyle captured on his Roam "Trends" page: **pay attention to the trends coming out of large public companies and the crossover hedge funds tracking them** — reading public-market conviction (what a Coatue-style manager thinks AI will do to Intel or Twitter, where a tech-billionaire hedge-fund manager is concentrating) as a leading signal for private-market themes.
