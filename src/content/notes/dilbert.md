# Dilbert

[Scott Adams](/notes/scott-adams)' comic strip, named by [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant) in [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant) alongside [Calvin and Hobbes](/notes/calvin-and-hobbes) as a work so authentically its creator's own that it can't be out-competed: "Is somebody else going to come along and write a better Dilbert? No. Is someone going to compete with [Bill Watterson](/notes/bill-watterson) and create a better Calvin and Hobbes? No." Purely a passing example within Naval's "escape competition through authenticity" argument — you can't out-compete work that is a genuine extension of who its maker is.
