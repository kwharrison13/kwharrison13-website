# Scope Expansion

[Keith Rabois](/notes/keith-rabois)'s management practice of continually expanding an employee's responsibilities 'until it breaks' — the level just below the break point is the role they should hold. A recurring driver of growth inside talent vortexes.

One of the two frameworks (with [Barrels and Ammunition](/notes/barrels-and-ammunition)) that The Talent Vortex - Research draws from Rabois's "How to Operate" talk (Lecture 14), later summarized by [Delian Asparouhov](/notes/delian-asparouhov) in his *Lessons from Keith Rabois* series. "Constant scope expansion" anchors one of the four parts of the published essay's synthesis — the mechanism by which a [talent vortex](/notes/talent-vortex) grows its people faster than a conventional org.
