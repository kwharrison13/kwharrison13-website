# Ed Rothschild

A Harvard Law School classmate of [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s, mentioned in passing in [Charlie Munger — The Psychology of Human Misjudgment](/notes/charlie-munger-the-psychology-of-human-misjudgment) as Munger reflects on how an old man "popping off" outside his formal training risks perceptions of arrogance. Munger credits Rothschild with the phrase **"the shoe button complex"** — named for a family friend who "spoke in oracular style on all subjects after becoming dominant in the shoe button business." No other biographical detail is given in the source.

## Where this appears

- [Charlie Munger — The Psychology of Human Misjudgment](/notes/charlie-munger-the-psychology-of-human-misjudgment) — Munger references his "old Harvard Law classmate, Ed Rothschild" and the "shoe button complex" while discussing how unconventional views are received.
