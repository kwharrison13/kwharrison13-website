# Photography Tours For Photographers

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Find photographers who have worked in beautiful locations for a long time and know the area and can take photography students or amateurs on those tours. Similar to the photography conference that chad and Denise went to, just giving photographers a chance to diversify their portfolios
- Add to photography walks, historical photo reenactments. Tours to locations of historical photos where you can see how they were set up, take your own photo of the location now and be given information on how to set up a reenactment shoot if you're interested
