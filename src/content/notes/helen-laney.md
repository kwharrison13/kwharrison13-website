# Helen Laney

**One-line:** *Kyle's great-grandmother — daughter of [William Sheppard Laney](/notes/william-sheppard-laney) and [Sara Edna Adair](/notes/sara-edna-adair); married [Patrick Allen Thompson](/notes/patrick-allen-thompson), carrying the Luna line from Laney into Thompson.*

## What we know

- **Lived 1914–2004** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Daughter of [William Sheppard Laney](/notes/william-sheppard-laney) (1877–1958) and [Sara Edna Adair](/notes/sara-edna-adair) (1881–1918)** — her mother died when Helen was ~4 (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [Patrick Allen Thompson](/notes/patrick-allen-thompson) (1911–1978)**; mother of **[Patricia Thompson](/notes/patricia-thompson)** (living), Kyle's grandmother (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
