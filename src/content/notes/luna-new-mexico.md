# Luna, New Mexico

**One-line:** *Kyle's family town — an unincorporated community of ~150 on the New Mexico–Arizona border, settled by [Mormon](/notes/mormon) ranchers in 1883 and home to five generations of Kyle's family.*

The shared **place page** for the [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing) program — everything about the town itself (history, geography, current status) hangs here so the venture and history pages can link in rather than restate it.

## What we know

- **Population ~150**, on the New Mexico–Arizona border (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)).
- **Named after [Solomon Luna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Luna)**, at one point the wealthiest man in New Mexico — but **settled by Mormon ranchers in 1883** (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)).
- **[William Laney Jr.](/notes/william-laney-jr)**, Kyle's great-great-great grandfather, was among those 1883 settlers — born in [Salt Lake City](/notes/salt-lake) in 1848, asked at age 35 to move with a group of Saints to settle in New Mexico (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)). Per Kyle's organization brief, he was sent by [Wilford Woodruff](/notes/wilford-woodruff) to help settle Luna.
- **Five generations** of Laneys / Thompsons / Shupes have lived in Luna since; the family still owns property there, which Kyle will inherit (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns), organization brief).
- Kyle's daughter is named **Eve Luna Harrison** (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)).
- Kyle frames Luna as the anchor of his [City Building](/notes/city-building) portfolio idea and wants "to find ways to help it thrive" — the seed of [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing) (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns)).

## Current status

- **Unincorporated.** A workstream of [The Luna Landing](/notes/the-luna-landing) is moving the town toward becoming an [Incorporated Village](/notes/incorporated-village) — see [Municipal Incorporation (NM)](/notes/municipal-incorporation-nm).
