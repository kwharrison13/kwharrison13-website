# Sara Edna Adair

**One-line:** *Kyle's great-great-grandmother — wife of [William Sheppard Laney](/notes/william-sheppard-laney); died young in 1918, holding no federal land patent in her own name.*

## What we know

- **Lived 1881–1918** — died at ~37 (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Daughter of Samuel Newton Adair (1839–1924) and Helen Genett Brown (1845–1933)** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [William Sheppard Laney](/notes/william-sheppard-laney) (1877–1958)**; mother of **[Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney)** (1914–2004) (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **No federal land patent exists in her name** — a full search of NM Adair and Laney patent rolls returned nothing. Any land interest she held would have been a community-property interest in her husband's patented ground or a later county-level deed; those records are in the Catron County Clerk's books, not the federal index (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).

## Open questions

- Her interest and life remain undocumented at the federal level; confirming would require Catron/Socorro County deeds + probate. A candidate subject for The Luna Novel — the young death (1918) makes her a natural "two very different lives" counterpart to her husband.
