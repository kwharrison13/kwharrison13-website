# Tyler Tringas

**One-line:** *Founder of [Calm Fund](/notes/calm-fund); a bootstrapped cleantech founder (started ~2010, couldn't get funding, bootstrapped and sold to PE) who now runs a research-heavy fund built around a rigorous 5-page investment brief.*

## What we know

- Began his career in cleantech around 2010, when "nobody wanted to fund that stuff." Unable to raise, he bootstrapped the business and sold it to PE ~2017. (source: October 4th, 2022 — Tyler Tringas (Calm Fund))
- Now runs [Calm Fund](/notes/calm-fund), which backs bootstrapped / capital-efficient software businesses. He frames funds as facing three bottlenecks — **capital, deal flow, and GP bandwidth**.
- Calm runs a significantly longer application process than [Y Combinator](/notes/y-combinator)'s: a research team produces a rigorous 5-page brief so the fund knows it wants to invest before ever getting on the phone — at which point "it's the founder's to lose."
