# Sarah Lacy

Named only in passing as one of the interviewers whose conversations with [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant) were excerpted to build [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant). Editor [Eric Jorgenson](/notes/eric-jorgenson) notes that "many excerpts are from interviews by fantastic creators like [Shane Parrish](/notes/shane-parrish), Sarah Lacy, [Joe Rogan](/notes/joe-rogan), and [Tim Ferriss](/notes/tim-ferriss)," and that for continuity he does not distinguish the various interviewers from each other. The book says nothing more about her specifically.
