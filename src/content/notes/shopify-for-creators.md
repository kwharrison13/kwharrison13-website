# Shopify for Creators

An entity-idea Kyle tagged in his Roam **Deep Dives** research (in the [E-Commerce Tech Roll-Up](/notes/e-commerce-tech-roll-up) bucket): a [Shopify](/notes/shopify)-style commerce/infrastructure layer purpose-built for individual creators rather than traditional merchants — arming the [Digital Creators](/notes/digital-creators) class with the storefront, payments, and back-office tooling to monetize a direct audience.

It sits at the intersection of two of Kyle's recurring theses. From [Digital Creators](/notes/digital-creators): the internet collapses gatekeepers and lets individual personalities become brands. From Picks and Shovels — Deep Dive: once Shopify is the API ecosystem, the opportunity is to build the specialized services (retail, chat, returns, manufacturing, etc.) that serve those merchants — and, by extension, creators. It's also a flavor of the [Business-in-a-Box](/notes/business-in-a-box) idea: give a technology-enabled middle class its own front-end rather than routing everyone through one marketplace.
