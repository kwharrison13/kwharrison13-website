# Programmatic SEO

**One-line:** *Generating templated, individually-indexable landing pages across a structured dimension space (location × category × attribute) to own thousands of long-tail search queries — the top-of-funnel moat behind marketplaces like Zillow, Tripadvisor, and FishingBooker.*

A durable growth concept surfaced in the [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) research. The reusable playbook for a listings/marketplace business where **data is the moat**.

## How sources describe it

- **The moat is data + coverage:** templated pages across a dimension space (for Gila Hunts: **unit × species × hunt-type × season**, plus draw-odds/success-rate data pages) rank for the long-tail queries users actually type (source: [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) market research).
- **FishingBooker is the canonical example:** its durable moat is programmatic local-SEO pages ("fishing charters in [city]") ranking for thousands of long-tail queries; it solved supply by doing each captain's marketing for them.
- **Single-player hook:** the data pages (draw-odds/unit guides) pull users in *before* the network exists, then route warm leads to listed suppliers — decoupling top-of-funnel from the cold-start problem.
- **Stack implication:** demands server-rendered HTML with full URL/canonical/schema control (why Gila Hunts chose Next.js over no-code Sharetribe/Bubble, which can't deliver it).
- **Heads are saturated, long-tail is winnable:** compete on unit/species-specific terms where incumbents have weak, outdated sites — not on generic head terms.
