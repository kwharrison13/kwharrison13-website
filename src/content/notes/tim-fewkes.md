# Tim Fewkes

Person Kyle references in his note on [Meaningful Travel](/notes/meaningful-travel): Tim and [Karen Fewkes](/notes/karen-fewkes) take their kids to [Africa](/notes/africa) each summer to pull them out of the reality they're accustomed to — a lived example of the essay's travel-as-transformation theme.

He also gave a sacrament-meeting talk (August 29, 2021) — Seeking Truth to Relieve Suffering — Tim Fewkes — teaching that seeking out [Truth](/notes/truth) is how we alleviate suffering, and that relief only lasts when it is grounded in truth. Kyle recorded his line: "It took me 40 years to learn that my job is to relieve suffering, no matter the cause."
