# LinkedIn

**One-line definition:** *The professional network co-founded by [Reid Hoffman](/notes/reid-hoffman); a PayPal-alumni company later acquired by [Microsoft](/notes/microsoft), and a recurring example across Kyle's notes on writing culture and incumbent distribution.*

## How it shows up

- **Writing culture** (The Storytelling of Investing - Research) — LinkedIn is cited alongside [Stripe](/notes/stripe) and [Shopify](/notes/shopify) as a company that runs a process to summarize customer feedback weekly (e.g. an NPS team), part of the [Wordsmiths](/notes/wordsmiths) thread on institutionalizing the summary of customer feedback.
- **PayPal lineage** ([The Founders](/books/the-founders)) — one of the defining firms seeded by early [PayPal](/notes/paypal) employees (the [Talent Vortex](/notes/talent-vortex)), listed alongside YouTube, [Tesla](/notes/tesla), and Palantir.
- **Hoffman's thesis** ([The Start-Up of You](/books/the-start-up-of-you)) — the book's professional-network framing mirrors the company [Reid Hoffman](/notes/reid-hoffman) co-founded.
- **Distribution over standalone value** ([Salesforce Acquires Slack, Salesforce's Reasoning, Salesforce's Opportunity](/notes/salesforce-acquires-slack-salesforce-s-reasoning-salesforce-s-opportunity)) — the [Microsoft](/notes/microsoft) acquisition is used as a precedent: LinkedIn was *"a valuable asset that couldn't make it on its own, but which Microsoft with its scale could grow into a valuable business"* — valuable **because of** Microsoft's distribution.
