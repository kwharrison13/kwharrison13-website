# Harriet Ellen Fuller

**One-line:** *Kyle's great-great-great grandmother — wife of [William Laney Jr.](/notes/william-laney-jr), one of the founding Laney couple at [Luna](/notes/luna-new-mexico).*

## What we know

- **Lived 1852–1932** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [William Laney Jr.](/notes/william-laney-jr)** (1848–1922); pictured with him in the Family Towns essay ("William Laney Jr. and Harriet Ellen Fuller Laney") (source: [Family Towns](/essays/family-towns), [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- Mother of **[William Sheppard Laney](/notes/william-sheppard-laney)** (1877–1958), the next Laney in the direct line at Luna.
