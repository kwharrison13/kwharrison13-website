# QuickStop

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- a platform that you can type in your start point and destination and it will give you a step by step guide to the series of public transportation you'll need to use even to either displaying your train ticket to be scanned or telling you exactly what cash you'll need. Then as you go through it you can check off each step
- an app that leverages google maps or apple maps but as it maps your route, it will tell you everything along the way, buildings you pass, what they are, any event info about them or discounts/deals/coupons for them.
