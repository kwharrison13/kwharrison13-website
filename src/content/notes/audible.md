# Audible

[Amazon](/notes/amazon)'s audiobook platform. In Kyle's corpus it appears twice: as the medium through which he listened to [Michelle Obama](/notes/michelle-obama)'s 2019 memoir [Becoming](/books/becoming) (a format/provenance tag on that book page), and in the long-read [It's Time To Disrupt Our Archaic Education System](/notes/it-s-time-to-disrupt-our-archaic-education-system), where it is grouped with [YouTube](/notes/youtube), Coursera, Udacity, Lynda, and [Khan Academy](/notes/khan-academy) as one of the free/low-cost channels giving unprecedented access to "the sharpest minds in the world" — the counterweight to rising tuition. It is not itself a topic discussed at length.

## Where this appears

- [Becoming](/books/becoming) — the audiobook platform via which Kyle consumed Michelle Obama's memoir in 2019.
- [It's Time To Disrupt Our Archaic Education System](/notes/it-s-time-to-disrupt-our-archaic-education-system) — listed among the platforms democratizing access to education.
