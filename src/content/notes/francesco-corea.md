# Francesco Corea

**One-line:** *At [Balderton Capital](/notes/balderton-capital); built its in-house research function — a data tech stack plus a two-year research-analyst program feeding the investment team.*

Corea's role split two ways: a **data tech stack** (curating the tools the investment team uses and leveraging data for sourcing) and a **research function** modeled on investment banking, where a two-year cohort of analysts run 4–8 week deep dives so any investor can "jump on a call with a founder not knowing anything about the space but with 30 minutes of prep." Output is a 30–50 page deck plus an investment tracker; coverage follows a **"T-Approach"** — broad across many companies, then narrowing into deep coverage of specific sectors — where the hard, unsolved piece is **taxonomy** (he used [Neobanks](/notes/neobanks) as the canonical example of how sector categorization fails to surface the truly competitive companies). Corea first heard about [Contrary](/notes/contrary) through [Ross Mason](/notes/ross-mason), the [MuleSoft](/notes/mulesoft) founder and a Contrary LP; the call was framed as a reaction to [Contrary Research](/essays/contrary-research) and how it compares to [Equity Research](/notes/equity-research) in public markets.
