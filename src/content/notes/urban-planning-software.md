# Urban Planning Software

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- The biggest problem with cases like “Madeupopolis” is that they hand a brief of the project to the architect and he draws up some pretty pictures of what it looks like. The better way is to bring all the stakeholders together and consider every need from multiple perspectives.
- We need a SIM city program that operates on statistics and rules of urban planning, design and statistics but it has a “forward” button. You map out the exact specifications of the city you’re working on, insert the changes you want to make and then hit the “forward” button and jump 1-50 years ahead and observe the changes. Rather than randomly selecting a path to follow, the program will walk you through the consequences and any number of possibilities based on statistic probability.
