# Consumerization

**One-line definition:** *Grove's observation that the computer industry shifted to serving consumers spending their own discretionary money, who hold the same expectations for computers that they hold for other household goods — a much larger but much tougher market than the old enterprise-driven one.* — [Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive)

## How sources describe it

- "The computer industry has largely become one that services consumers who use their own discretionary spending to purchase a product, and who apply the same expectations to those products that they have for other household goods. [Intel](/notes/intel) has had to start adjusting to this new reality... The good news is, we all have a much larger market. The bad news is, it is a much tougher market than we were accustomed to servicing." Kyle's inline note labels this "Consumerization of everything." ([Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive))

## Where it shows up

- [Only the Paranoid Survive](/books/only-the-paranoid-survive) — named as one of the forces reshaping Intel's environment alongside the rise of the internet.
