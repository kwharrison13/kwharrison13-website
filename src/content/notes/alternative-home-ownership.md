# Alternative Home Ownership

New models for owning a home — rent-to-own, fractional ownership, iBuying, mortgage-tech, and micro real estate that sit between renting and traditional purchase. Kyle flagged it on his Roam "Trends" idea-capture page (linked from his personal [Real Estate](/notes/real-estate) hub) and it folds in the linked Roam "Alternative Home Ownership" page, marked done on ingest. Companion to [Home Buying](/notes/home-buying) and [Alternative Real Estate](/notes/alternative-real-estate).
