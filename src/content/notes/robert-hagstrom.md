# Robert Hagstrom

Author of [The Warren Buffett Portfolio](/notes/the-warren-buffett-portfolio), a book in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read). The book argues for *focus investing* — concentrating a portfolio in a few thoroughly understood, high-quality businesses rather than diversifying broadly, drawing on [Warren Buffett](/notes/warren-buffett)'s methods. It surfaced on Kyle's list because it appears on [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s recommended-reading list in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack).
