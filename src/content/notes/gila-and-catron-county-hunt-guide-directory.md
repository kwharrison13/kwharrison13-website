# Gila Wilderness / Catron County — Hunt Guide & Outfitter Reference

**One-line:** *A tiered, sourced directory of outfitters guiding in the Gila Wilderness and greater Gila National Forest (Catron-County focus), plus how hunt-guide "ratings" actually work — the supply-side map for both [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts) (the marketplace) and the [Luna hunting/retreat](/notes/luna-hunting-economy) partner-outfitter play.*

> **Why this is here:** the outfitter supply side feeds [Hunt Outfitter Industry](/notes/hunt-outfitter-industry), is the competitor/supply map for [Gila Hunts](/notes/gila-hunts), and is the partner shortlist for the retreat venture's "partner with a Unit 16 outfitter holding landowner tags + USFS permit" option. **Verify licensing/permits/phones directly before booking or partnering.**

---

> **Compiled:** July 2026
> **Scope:** Outfitters guiding in the Gila Wilderness & greater Gila National Forest, southwestern New Mexico — with a focus on Catron County.
> **Sources:** Outfitter websites, NM Council of Outfitters & Guides directory, Sierra County tourism listings, USFS Gila NF, and hunter forums (Rokslide, Hunt Talk, ArcheryTalk, Long Range Hunting, TexasBowhunter).
> **Note:** Phone numbers, licensing, and permits change year to year — verify directly before booking. Where a field is `—`, it wasn't publicly confirmed at compile time.

## How hunt-guide "ratings" actually work

There is **no single "Yelp for hunt guides."** Remote outfitters like these often have few or zero Google/Yelp reviews, so a star average is a weak signal on its own. Reputation signals, ranked best-first:

| Signal / Source | Trust | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **First-hand references** (same unit + weapon) | Highest | Ask each outfitter for 3–5 recent clients and call them. The gold standard. |
| **Hunting forums** (Rokslide, Hunt Talk, ArcheryTalk, Long Range Hunting, TexasBowhunter) | High | Unfiltered good-and-bad. Where the Gila locals built their names — and where bad outfits get flagged. |
| **NM Council of Outfitters & Guides + NMDGF licensed list** | High (verification) | Confirms licensed, bonded, insured, and permitted. Being unlisted is a red flag. |
| **USFS Gila NF special-use permit** | High (verification) | Legit Gila outfitters hold a Forest Service permit. Confirm via Gila NF Supervisor's Office: **575-388-8201**. |
| **Google Business reviews** | Medium — best *universal star* metric, but sparse | Closest cross-comparable star rating; many outfits here have <10 or none. First star number to check. |
| **Endorsement services** (Huntin' Fool/HFA, GOHUNT, Rolling Bones, Jim Shockey's Book Your Hunt) | Medium | Useful shortlists, but outfitters often **pay** for placement — "pre-screened," not "ranked." |
| **Celebrity endorsements** (e.g., Craig Boddington) | Low–Medium | Marketing weight; forum veterans warn some sketchy outfits carry them. |
| **Guidefitter** | Low for outfitter ratings | Good directory + pro community, but primarily gear-review/booking, not a consumer star system. |
| **Facebook followers / engagement** | Low (popularity ≠ quality) | Interest proxy only. Read the actual comments/tagged photos. |
| **B&C / P&Y record-book claims** | Context | Catron Co genuinely fills record books — that's true of the *area*, not proof of the *guide*. Ask for their photos, dates, units, scores. |

**Recommended workflow:** shortlist by tier + species + unit → verify NM license + Gila NF permit → pull Google stars *and* forum search the name → call 3–5 recent references for your exact unit/weapon → confirm draw-pool mechanics (several require booking to enter the outfitter draw pool).
