# Robbie Shattuck

**One-line:** *Wealth-management investor at ATHOS — a fund-of-funds + co-invest shop — who previously managed [Chris Cox](/notes/chris-cox)'s account at [ICONIQ](/notes/iconiq).*

Kyle spoke with Shattuck on a February 1st, 2022 [Contrary Capital](/notes/contrary) call about ATHOS's strategy: an 85%-fund-of-funds early-stage book plus opportunistic co-investment, graduating its biggest clients from the ATHOS vehicle into direct checks. He managed [Chris Cox](/notes/chris-cox)'s account at [ICONIQ](/notes/iconiq) (no longer there) and first met Cox in 2014 as Cox was coming into money; it was through Cox that ATHOS heard about [Contrary Capital](/notes/contrary). He later resurfaces as a referral: a JP Morgan private-bank friend of [Alec Anderson](/notes/alec-anderson)'s (also [ICONIQ](/notes/iconiq)) was described as "close to Robbie Shattuck."
