# Elliot Bay Book Company

The Elliott Bay Book Company is an independent bookstore in Seattle's Capitol Hill neighborhood. It surfaces across Kyle's wiki as a recurring recommender — the source of a cluster of books he picked up and read in 2017: [Speeches That Changed The World](/books/speeches-that-changed-the-world), [In Defense of a Liberal Education](/books/in-defense-of-a-liberal-education), [Islam & The Future of Tolerance](/books/islam-and-the-future-of-tolerance), and [Me and Earl and The Dying Girl](/books/me-and-earl-and-the-dying-girl).
