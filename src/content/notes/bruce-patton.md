# Bruce Patton

Co-author (with [Roger Fisher](/notes/roger-fisher) and [William Ury](/notes/william-ury)) of [Getting to Yes](/notes/getting-to-yes), the negotiation classic in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read). The book — which lays out "principled negotiation" (separate the people from the problem, focus on interests not positions, invent mutually beneficial options judged by objective criteria) — reached Kyle's list via [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack), part of [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s perennial reading list.

**Context:** Bruce Patton is a co-founder of the Harvard Negotiation Project and a deputy director there; he co-authored *Getting to Yes* and later co-wrote *Difficult Conversations*.
