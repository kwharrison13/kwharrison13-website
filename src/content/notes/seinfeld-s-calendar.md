# Seinfeld's Calendar

Passing reference: tagged as a bare hashtag (alongside a [Daily Routine](/notes/daily-routine) tag) onto a passage in [How To Take Smart Notes](/books/how-to-take-smart-notes) about [Anthony Trollope](/notes/anthony-trollope)'s daily writing ritual — starting every morning at 5:30 a.m. and writing at least 250 words every 15 minutes, "over ten pages of an ordinary novel volume a day." The tag draws an implicit parallel to the "don't break the chain" daily-consistency method, but the text itself doesn't name or explain the technique. The book frames Trollope's steady-accumulation approach as a piggy-bank next to Luhmann's slip-box (compounding interest).
