# Arthur Herman

Author of [How the Scots Invented the Modern World](/notes/how-the-scots-invented-the-modern-world), which argues that Scottish Enlightenment thinkers pioneered the ideas of modernity — capitalism, democracy, science, and individualism — that shaped the Western world. The book is in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read) and was recommended in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack) as part of [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s reading list.
