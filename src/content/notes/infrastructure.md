# Infrastructure

Umbrella bucket in Kyle's Roam **Deep Dives** research for the layer beneath the application — the data, compute, and tooling substrate that software runs on. In the Deep Dives index the "infrastructure" heading grouped the two sub-topics Kyle was tracking most closely: Data Infrastructure (the modern data stack — Fivetran, Snowflake, streaming) and [Infrastructure-as-Code](/notes/infrastructure-as-code) (declarative provisioning).

It's the connective tissue for a good chunk of his Enterprise Software — Deep Dive work, where infra trends (containers/Kubernetes/microservices, Infrastructure-as-Code) sit at the top of the "Trends" list.
