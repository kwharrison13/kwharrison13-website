# Frank Partnoy

Author of [F.I.A.S.C.O.](/notes/f-i-a-s-c-o), a book in Kyle's anti-library (books-to-read).

**Context:** A former [Morgan Stanley](/notes/morgan-stanley) derivatives salesman. His book *F.I.A.S.C.O.: Blood in the Water on Wall Street* is an insider exposé of the predatory culture and complex financial instruments driving 1990s Wall Street. It surfaced in Kyle's notes because it appears on [Charlie Munger](/notes/charlie-munger)'s recommended-reading list in [Poor Charlie's Almanack](/books/poor-charlie-s-almanack).
