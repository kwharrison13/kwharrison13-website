# Traffic Buses & Zip Cars

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Create a system where people can sign up their location and find a bus route that they can get picked up somewhere (just use publicly available, but not company owned parking areas) to meet, they get in the bus and everyone in the bus (like an UberPool) is going in the same general direction.
- There could also be stops along the way that have small zip cars, or other small rental services to make it easier for the bus to stop in one central location and people can then get where they’re going.
- The sign up page (probably online) would let you put in your home address, your destination, the kind of car you drive, when you usually leave, etc. and it will calculate a cost based not only on your information but on the increased safety, convenience, and time of your ride. (For example, for every one less car on the road, that increases your chances of surviving by 0.00001% and if a life is worth $10M then that ride SHOULD be worth $100. That can be more informational than it actually worked into the cost.
- The buses could be fairly nice, and it wouldn’t be set routes like city buses. It would be mapped out routes based on rider needs, like UberPool.
