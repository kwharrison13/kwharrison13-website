# Delian Asparouhov

Investor at [Founders Fund](/notes/founders-fund) (a partner alongside [Keith Rabois](/notes/keith-rabois) in the firm's PayPal-Mafia orbit) who is "himself building a space manufacturing company." He writes the "Operator" Substack and shows up across Kyle's corpus as both a talent/venture thinker and a deep-tech operator.

Grounded appearances:

- **Lessons from Keith Rabois.** Author of the *Lessons from Keith Rabois* essay series; Kyle draws on Essay 5, [How to Become a Magnet for Talent and Assess Talent](http://delian.io/lessons-5), in The Talent Vortex - Research — the strand that feeds Rabois's [Barrels and Ammunition](/notes/barrels-and-ammunition) and [Scope Expansion](/notes/scope-expansion) frameworks. (One note flags Delian's summary as "not as in-depth a framework as others.")
- **Deep tech / venture economics.** In [Deep Tech For Deep Minds Or Deep Pockets](/essays/deep-tech-for-deep-minds-or-deep), Kyle cites Delian on the shifting dynamics of traditional venture: software companies now compete with cloud-native incumbents, so "that zero marginal cost… suddenly becomes zero margin."
- **Generational and cultural commentary.** Kyle logs a Delian stat on the share of kids who want to be a YouTuber (The Rising Generation - Research) and a Delian tweet on excess/hubris (The Existential Dread of Cognitive Dissonance - Research).
- **Media surface.** His Operator Substack is noted as a reference source in Open-Source Knowledge - Research.
