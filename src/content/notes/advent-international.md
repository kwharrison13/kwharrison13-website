# Advent International

**One-line definition:** *Global private equity firm; co-led [Salt Security](/notes/salt-security)'s $70M Series C alongside [Alkeon](/notes/alkeon) (May 2021, $625M post).*

In Kyle's Salt Security Diligence — 2021 dossier, Advent and [Alkeon](/notes/alkeon) together led the $70M Series C at a $625M post-money — the round that followed [Tenaya Capital](/notes/tenaya-capital)'s Series A and [Sequoia](/notes/sequoia)'s $30M Series B (Dec 2020). Bryan Taylor, managing partner and head of Advent's Palo Alto technology team, was quoted in the funding press release on the API-security opportunity: "The rapid proliferation of APIs has dramatically altered the attack surface of applications, creating a major challenge for large enterprises since existing security mechanisms cannot protect against this new threat."

## Where it shows up
