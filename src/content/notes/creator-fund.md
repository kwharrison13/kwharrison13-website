# Creator Fund

**One-line definition:** *European VC ([thecreatorfund.com](https://www.thecreatorfund.com/)) that uses students to scout deals; raised a $20M fund to expand across the rest of Europe.*

Surfaced as a European parallel to [Contrary](/notes/contrary)'s student-scout model: [Francesco Corea](/notes/francesco-corea) of [Balderton Capital](/notes/balderton-capital) cited it when reacting to [Contrary Research](/essays/contrary-research), noting the Creator Fund "has been using students to scout for them" and had "just raised [a] $20M fund to expand to the rest of Europe over the next few years."
