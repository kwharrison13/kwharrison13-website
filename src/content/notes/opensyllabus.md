# OpenSyllabus

Bare `#OpenSyllabus` tag in *[How To Take Smart Notes](/books/how-to-take-smart-notes)*, attached (alongside #[Syllabus 2.0](/notes/syllabus-2-0)) to the passage arguing that prearranging information into modules, categories, and themes for students backfires — it takes away the effort of building meaningful connections and translating material into one's own language, which is what real [Learning](/notes/learning) requires (*"the one who does the work does the learning"*). The source doesn't explain what OpenSyllabus itself is; the tag appears to mark the passage as relevant to that idea rather than to describe it. Left thin until a grounded source defines it.
