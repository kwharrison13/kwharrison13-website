## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Set up a data tracker that will automatically pull in (e.g.) the ten most common flights from the ten most traveled airlines every day and see how the prices change, based on time before flight. (e.g. what does it look like 10 hours before the flight vs. 10 days vs. 10 weeks)
- Whenever we buy airline tickets, make sure to call right before the flights and say "I want to transfer it over to my friend" and see what they say.
- Do research on the marketing spend/strategy that it would take to build a following that people trust
