# J.D. Rockefeller

A passing historical name-drop in [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant)'s discussion of technology and wealth. Illustrating [Naval Ravikant](/notes/naval-ravikant)'s point that "almost everything in your house, in your workplace, and on the street used to be technology at one point in time," the text cites oil as "a technology that made J.D. Rockefeller rich," paired with a parallel mention of cars making [Henry Ford](/notes/henry-ford) rich. No further detail is given.
