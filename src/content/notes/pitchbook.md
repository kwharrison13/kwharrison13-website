# PitchBook

**One-line definition:** *Private-markets data provider whose editorial arm, PitchBook News, covers venture and PE — for Kyle it functions both as a circa-2017 reading source and, more often, as the authoritative dataset he cites when he needs a number.*

Across Kyle's corpus PitchBook shows up in two modes. First, as **media**: PitchBook News sits in the deal-news cluster of his 2017 startup-media inventory (The State of Startup Media - Research), grouped with [PE Hub](/notes/pe-hub), [StrictlyVC](/notes/strictlyvc), Axios Pro Rata, and Fortune Term Sheet — the more institutional "before" snapshot the essay [The State of Startup Media](/essays/the-state-of-startup-media) measures the post-COVID media explosion against. Second, and more consequentially, as a **data source of record**: when Kyle reaches for a hard private-markets figure, the citation is usually PitchBook.

## Where this appears

- The State of Startup Media - Research — *PitchBook News* in the 2017 inventory, deal-news cluster.
- [Mo' Money, Mo' Problems](/essays/mo-money-mo-problems) — cited as the research firm behind SoftBank Vision Fund AUM figures and (via the PitchBook–NVCA Venture Monitor report) the finding that LPs "have become more selective and cautious" as average funding fell.
- November 7th, 2022 — Cleared Email — PitchBook data pegging Tiger Global's John Curtius as having led 111 of the firm's deals (vs. 54 for Scott Shleifer, 32 for Griffin Schroeder).
