# Homebrew

**One-line:** *Early-stage venture firm co-founded by [Hunter Walk](/notes/hunter-walk) and Satya Patel in 2013, known for a hands-on, small-check-count model — and, since 2022, for restructuring its fourth fund as an evergreen vehicle funded solely by its own partners' capital.*

## What we know

- Founded by [Hunter Walk](/notes/hunter-walk) and Satya Patel, who met while both working at Google pre-IPO; raised its first $35M seed-stage fund in 2013 to invest in the "bottom-up economy" (source: [Renegade Spotlight — Homebrew](/essays/homebrew)).
- Named after the Homebrew Computer Club, a late-1970s Menlo Park hobbyist club Steve Wozniak credited as the inspiration for the first Apple I — not, per the essay, a coffee reference.
- Core value proposition has held for ~10 years: a hands-on seed fund writing a small number of checks so it can stay closely involved with portfolio companies, explicitly framing "entrepreneurs as our customers."
- Raised three core funds totaling ~$200M with strong LP demand; early-stage investments include [Plaid](/notes/plaid), Gusto, and Cruise.
- In February 2022 announced "Homebrew IV(ever)": its fourth fund is an evergreen vehicle composed solely of Walk's and Patel's own capital — no outside LPs, no fixed investment horizon, no fund-size-driven AUM incentives — deliberately "zigging while the market zags" rather than raising a larger traditional fund.
- Replaced its single "seed investor of record" product with a broader mandate: relationships with founders regardless of company stage, no check-size limits, no ownership targets, no fundraising-stage requirements.
- Walk described the firm's differentiated approach in a co-authored 2022 blog post ("Homebrew Forever: Same Mission, Our Capital"): getting founders "to the right answer faster," and measuring itself by whether founders can point to something concrete Homebrew did for them in the last 30 days (source: Renegade Spotlight: Homebrew - Research).
- One of the ten founding partner firms behind Screendoor, a $50M fund-of-funds backing underrepresented emerging VCs, alongside Cowboy Ventures, Fika Ventures, Forerunner Ventures, Fuel Capital, Kindred Ventures, Precursor Ventures, and Reach Capital.
