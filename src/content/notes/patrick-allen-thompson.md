# Patrick Allen Thompson

**One-line:** *Kyle's great-grandfather — married [Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney), bringing the Thompson name into the Luna line.*

## What we know

- **Lived 1911–1978** (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- **Married [Helen Laney](/notes/helen-laney) (1914–2004)**; father of **[Patricia Thompson](/notes/patricia-thompson)** (living), Kyle's grandmother (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
- His descendants are among the present-day Thompson owners recorded in Section 5, T6S R20W near the old Laney ground (source: [Laney Family Land at Luna](/notes/laney-family-land-at-luna)).
