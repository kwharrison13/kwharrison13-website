# Day in the Life (Travel App)

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- An app where you can put in your destination, pick the things you like, and it will que your day for you without you having to find the perfect thing to do.
- It can also monitor your day in real time so if it was going to send you to a beach or an ice cream shop but for whatever reason, they’re too crowded now, it will redirect you to something less crowded.
- You can also pay for things right in the app (reservations, tickets, etc.) or for a premium, it can even find people to be ready to help you for activities later on in your day (e.g. tour guides)
- Inspired by a combination of the San Francisco city cards and Hemispheres, the United Airlines travel show
- You could have sponsored days where a travel blogger will plan your day or a famous person or the bucket list family or things like that.
