# Craft Ventures

The early-stage venture firm [David Sacks](/notes/david-sacks) co-founded (with Bill Lee) in his post-[PayPal](/notes/paypal)/Yammer act. Craft is the affiliation behind [The Burn Multiple](/notes/the-burn-multiple) — Sacks's April 2020 essay defining Net Burn / Net New ARR as the single most revealing metric of a startup's [Capital Efficiency](/notes/capital-efficiency), which Kyle treats as the original definition of the [Burn Multiple](/notes/burn-multiple) concept.

Across Kyle's 2021–2024 diligence and sourcing notes, Craft recurs as an active investor and operator:

- **Koyfin** raised its seed round from Craft Ventures (Laney as lead investor) — November 15th, 2021 — Rob Koyfman (Koyfin).
- **[Arena.im](/notes/arena-im)** — Craft joined the Series A (CRV led), per March 30th, 2022 — Deal Announcements.
- **Portfolio content ops** — Craft uses Clipt.co, keeping two of its editors on retainer to make ads/video for portfolio companies on demand.
- **Talent as a service** — Craft was "starting to do something" on in-house recruiting/talent sourcing, a practice more common in PE (e.g. Vista Equity Partners) than venture — March 17th, 2022 — Matt Cassel (Growth Factors).
