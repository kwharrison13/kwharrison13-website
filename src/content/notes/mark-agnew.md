# Mark Agnew

Mark Agnew is a co-author (with [A.J. Wasserstein](/notes/a-j-wasserstein) and [Brian O'Connor](/notes/brian-o-connor)) of the Yale case study "On the Nature of Long-Term Holds" (May 28, 2020) that Kyle annotated in [On the Nature of Long-Term Holds](/notes/on-the-nature-of-long-term-holds). The case argues that a long-term hold is fundamentally a [Compounding](/notes/compounding) strategy — that entrepreneurs build more wealth by avoiding the taxes, transaction fees, idle time, and redeployment risk of trading, and by favoring multiple-on-invested-capital over IRR as their key metric.

## Where this appears

- [On the Nature of Long-Term Holds](/notes/on-the-nature-of-long-term-holds) — credited as a co-author of the Yale case study Kyle annotated on long-horizon ownership.
