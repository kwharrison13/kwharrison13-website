# Rikhil Patel

**One-line:** *Investor (VP) at [TCV](/notes/tcv) who led the firm's fall-2018 [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) category exploration; later at Brighton Park Capital.*

At [TCV](/notes/tcv), Patel worked the emerging [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) category alongside [Scott Kirk](/notes/scott-kirk) and Kyle. On Oct 2, 2018 he circulated Segment-partnerships notes framing the space as a "2-horse CDI race" between [Segment](/notes/segment) and [mParticle](/notes/mparticle) — and reached out to mParticle's CEO to open a meeting. His notes also captured the Segment "Synapse 2018" conference read and the AgilOne CEO's "struggling in their transition to the Enterprise" line. Earlier, in Jan 2018, he routed a Martech rollup thesis from Ryan Koonce (Mammoth Growth) to Kyle via David Yuan. In April 2019 he gave Kyle feedback on a [Figma](/notes/figma) investment case study prepared for a [Coatue](/notes/coatue) interview (underwrite to ~4x MOIC / 7x exit ARR; "focus the case on quality of business and growth of space").

He later moved to Brighton Park Capital. In a July 2022 call he walked Kyle through the FP&A / office-of-the-CFO landscape — his read on OneStream vs. [Anaplan](/notes/anaplan), whether a big business can be built on the planning layer alone, and people to talk to in that world. In a gratitude letter, Kyle names the 2018 CDP work with Patel as one of the formative early stretches of his investing education.
