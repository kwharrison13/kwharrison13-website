# Wayne Gretzky

Wayne Gretzky is the hockey player whose maxim — "Most players skate to where the puck is. I skate to where the puck is going to be" — [Angus King](/notes/angus-king) invokes in [Education Nation](/books/education-nation) to justify investing in students' digital futures. [Milton Chen](/notes/milton-chen) recounts King (then Governor of Maine, where "hockey is the closest thing to a state sport") deploying the line to defend one-to-one student laptops over roads and bridges, arguing Maine's future lay in a digital, scientifically literate workforce as industries like logging declined.

## Where this appears

- [Education Nation](/books/education-nation) — source of the "skate to where the puck is going to be" maxim, quoted by Governor [Angus King](/notes/angus-king) and recounted by [Milton Chen](/notes/milton-chen).
