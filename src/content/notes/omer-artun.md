# Omer Artun

**One-line:** *CEO of AgilOne, a full-stack B2C [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform).*

Kyle met Artun in a parallel CEO meeting in Sunnyvale during [TCV](/notes/tcv)'s fall-2018 [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) diligence (the effort anchored on [mParticle](/notes/mparticle)). AgilOne was a ~13-year-old full-stack CDP built exclusively for B2C brands; it had shifted from mid-market to Enterprise in 2014, narrowing its prospect list from ~40K to ~3K. Artun's positioning: "CDP is too complex for the mid-market" — smaller companies just need an email tool, so anyone serving them ends up "an email segmentation offering."

His full-stack thesis put identity resolution (stitching offline + online into a single customer view) as "the biggest problem," consuming 30–40% of dev resources, and argued a CDP can't just be a "dumb hub" — it needs an analytics engine plus an orchestration layer. On the competitive map he called [Redpoint](/notes/redpoint) the biggest competitor with the best product, said [mParticle](/notes/mparticle) and Tealium could "shuffle data but can't do analytics," and knocked [Segment](/notes/segment) for skewing B2B and lacking an orchestration layer.

On the business: AgilOne had raised ~$50M total, was at a ~$16M run-rate growing ~25% y/y and cash-flow positive, had retained Piper to help raise, and had TCV on its outreach list. Per Rikhil Patel's read at the time, the company was "struggling in their transition to the Enterprise."
