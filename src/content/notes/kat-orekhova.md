# Kat Orekhova

Co-Founder and CEO of [Vareto](/notes/vareto), a modern, enterprise-grade FP&A / financial planning and reporting platform, co-founded with [Lalit Singh](/notes/lalit-singh) (President) and [Nathan Harkenrider](/notes/nathan-harkenrider) (CTO). [Contrary](/notes/contrary) led Vareto's round; Kyle diligenced the deal.

**Background (per the Vareto diligence).** Before Vareto, Kat was Head of Product at Ironclad (scaling the business ~4× from Series B to C), and earlier built and led the revenue FP&A team at [Facebook](/notes/meta-company) — where she built highly accurate ML forecasting models (cited at under 1% quarterly variance over a ~15× growth period). She is also a GP at Darkmode Ventures, a ~$5M micro-fund, and authored Vareto's June 2023 investor memo (styled after Parker Conrad's Rippling "compound startup" memo).

**On the product.** Kat positioned Vareto's data-management foundations as "10x better than any other product out there" (framed against [Anaplan](/notes/anaplan)), targeting the reporting-and-close workflow first (dashboards, variance review, drill-down from tables into journal entries) and expanding into planning against legacy incumbents [Anaplan](/notes/anaplan) and [Adaptive Planning](/notes/adaptive-planning). As of her Dec 2022 update, Vareto had strong inbound investor interest and was targeting ~$1–2M ARR by spring 2023 to prove sales beyond founder-led.
