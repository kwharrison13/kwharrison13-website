# Michael Katz

**One-line:** *CEO and co-founder of [mParticle](/notes/mparticle); previously co-founded adtech business InterClick (IPO'd 2009, acquired by [Yahoo](/notes/yahoo) for $300M in 2011).*

Katz gave the initial CEO call in [TCV](/notes/tcv)'s fall-2018 [Customer Data Platform](/notes/customer-data-platform) diligence (Oct 30, 2018, with [Scott Kirk](/notes/scott-kirk), [Rikhil Patel](/notes/rikhil-patel), and Kyle). The mParticle product idea grew out of the founding team's InterClick experience and their time at [Yahoo](/notes/yahoo); they originally called the product "data automation," before the term "customer data platform" existed.

His framing of the category, as captured in Kyle's notes:

- **Two kinds of CDP.** Real-data CDPs (Treasure Data, [Segment](/notes/segment), [mParticle](/notes/mparticle)) that collect *primary* data and ingest any touchpoint in real time — versus everyone else, who ingest 3rd-party tools and focus on the front-end/application layer.
- **B2C CRM is up for grabs.** [Salesforce](/notes/salesforce) owns B2B CRM, but B2C CRM is "(1) very different and (2) owned by nobody." He saw CDP at "peak hype cycle," with many players riding the wave who weren't really CDPs.
- **Segment vs. mParticle.** He positioned [Segment](/notes/segment) as "much more focused on downstream customers" and argued the TAM was big enough for both.

On the business at the time: ~120 customers ("biggest and best" brands), ~$20M 2018 run-rate at just over 100% YoY growth, and ~150% net retention Yr1→Yr2; the Series B was just forming.
