# E-Commerce Penetration

The share of total retail that happens online — the secular growth curve that underwrites nearly all of Kyle's e-commerce work. He logged it as a tag in his Roam **Deep Dives** research (in the [E-Commerce Tech Roll-Up](/notes/e-commerce-tech-roll-up) / [Shopify](/notes/shopify) bucket), and it's the opening premise of his [E-Commerce](/notes/e-commerce) notes: *"% of retail moving online each year and how it's growing"* and *"infrastructure to support that."*

The thesis chain: as penetration rises, the merchant base and the volume flowing through platforms like [Shopify](/notes/shopify) grow with it, which expands the surface area for the picks-and-shovels tooling and the seller-software consolidation behind the [E-Commerce Tech Roll-Up](/notes/e-commerce-tech-roll-up) idea.
