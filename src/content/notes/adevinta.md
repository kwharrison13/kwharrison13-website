# Adevinta

_(Kyle's Roam "Deep Dives" page spelled this **Adventia** — that's a typo for **Adevinta**, the online-classifieds company. The bare-name alias resolves the original wikilink.)_

Adevinta shows up in Kyle's [E-Commerce](/notes/e-commerce) / [Unbundling](/notes/unbundling) research as the acquirer on the other side of eBay's classifieds exit. In the Deep Dives "Bundling and Unbundling" bucket, Kyle noted "[eBay](/notes/ebay) selling classifieds business to Adevinta" — a marquee example of a general-purpose marketplace shedding a vertical.

**Context:** Adevinta is a Norwegian online-classifieds group (spun out of Schibsted in 2019). In July 2020 it agreed to acquire [eBay](/notes/ebay)'s Classifieds Group for [$9.2 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2020/07/21/adevinta-buys-ebays-classifieds-unit.html) — $2.5B cash plus ~540M Adevinta shares — a deal (closed 2021) that made it the world's largest online-classifieds company. It runs marketplaces such as Leboncoin, mobile.de, and Kijiji, and was itself taken private by Permira and Blackstone in 2024.
