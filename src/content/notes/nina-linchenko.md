# Nina Linchenko

A reader in Kyle's orbit whose views on books and history recur across the corpus. She recommended [The Invention of Russia](/books/the-invention-of-russia) by [Arkady Ostrovsky](/notes/arkady-ostrovsky) to Kyle (recorded in the book page's people roster). On [We Need a New Science of Progress](/notes/we-need-a-new-science-of-progress) she left an emphatic endorsement — "Yes, yes, yes, yes, yes. This is everything I've ever wanted. More than ever, I'm convinced of the importance of historians outside of traditional academia." In Kyle's *Intrinsic Value* user research she is also quoted on *You Are a Badass* — it resonated but she never finished it, calling it "an information dump" better treated as a workbook, one principle at a time.
