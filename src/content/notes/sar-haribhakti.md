# Sar Haribhakti

Author/interviewer of [Not So Lazy & Entitled Millennials- David Perell](/notes/not-so-lazy-and-entitled-millennials-david-perell) (2018), the piece in which he interviews [David Perell](/notes/david-perell) about learning, writing, the "digital brain" ([Building a Second Brain](/notes/building-a-second-brain)), [Naked Brands](/notes/naked-brands), and the case for optimistic science fiction as fuel for innovation.

He surfaces again in Kyle's essay research on usage-based / deposit economics: a Haribhakti tweet (handle `sarthakgh`) is cited as a "meme" in the [Brex](/notes/brex)-deposit discussion collected in The Mysteries of an Economic Engine - Research.
