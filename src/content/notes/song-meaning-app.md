# Song Meaning App

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Create an App that can connect to iTunes, Spotify, etc. and as you’re listening to a song, it will ask you, “What does this song mean to you?” and its like a song journal. It can upload whatever you type, save it online, and post it to the website/app so if people want to look at a song meaning they can see other people’s responses
