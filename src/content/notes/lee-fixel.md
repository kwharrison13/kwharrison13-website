Founder of the venture firm [Addition](/notes/addition); previously a star investor at [Tiger Global](/notes/tiger-global-management). In [Stewards of Capital](/essays/stewards-of-capital), Kyle distinguishes Fixel from the "Tiger 2.0" archetype of massive markups and capital excess (the "excess we saw from the hedge fund in 2021") — characterizing him instead as making "very intellectually interesting bets," and Addition as a capable, thoughtful firm investing across construction, lending, and AI/ML. Addition (with True Ventures) led the $25M Series A in [John Foley](/notes/john-foley)'s [Ernesta](/notes/ernesta), the D2C custom-rug venture at the center of the essay; Kyle notes having co-invested alongside Addition and seen that thoughtfulness firsthand.
