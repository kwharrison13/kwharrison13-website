# Carol Loomis

Passing citation in [How To Take Smart Notes](/books/how-to-take-smart-notes), quoted on [writing](/notes/writing) as a thinking tool: "Writing itself makes you realise where there are holes in things. I'm never sure what I think until I see what I write... the analysis part of you kicks in when you sit down to construct a story or a paragraph or a sentence." The book gives no further detail about her.
