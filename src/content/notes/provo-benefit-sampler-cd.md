# Provo Benefit Sampler CD

> 💡 **Ideation-stage idea** — one of Kyle's [Entity Ideas](/notes/entity-ideas) business concepts, captured from his Roam notebook. Not an operating company; preserved here so the idea (and its research) survives.

## Idea capture (from Roam)

- Provo Benedit Sampler CD
- With Zach Collier and Brandon Porter
- Brain Dump By Brandon
- Alright, here's the byline: Create a sampler CD featuring three or five similar local artists. Each artist should contribute 2 to 4 of their best songs to the CD.
- There is some paperwork that we would have to have the artists sign, which would give us rights to their song SOLELY for the distribution of this CD. We would have to specify if we are intending to sell physical or electronic copies, or both.
- The usual distribution of money from profit is about 6 cents to each artist per song on the CD, per CD sold. I have to look more into it to make sure that's the best deal. We, as Sandbox, would then give each designated band leader a quarterly check for however much they are owed (Or we could work out a deal where we pay them every time we hit a threshold, like $25 or $50).
- So, to reiterate, Sandbox LLC would own the right to distribute the songs in question JUST for the CD (I know musicians get nervous when you talk about taking rights to music).
- Costs to Sandbox would include designing the CD cover, printing it, buying materials for the cases (potentially cardstock paper cases), buy the CDs themselves, and print on the CD. There is also the cost of mastering the CD, which I am looking around for someone who can do a decent job for cheap.
- Paperwork from bands, master the tracks, commission artwork, buy materials, create product, sell them.
- The ideal selling venue is at band concerts. Idea being, someone is listening to a band and thinks, "Wow! Provo has good music? Who knew?" They stare longingly at the merch table. "I wish I knew other great local bands." Then BOOM- they notice the Sampler CD, buy it, and they are that much more cultured.
- I'm not ready to lock down a final selling price yet. I want to know all our variable costs first and project our profit from there.
- Make sense? Brain has been dumped. Do with it as you will.
- Thanks Kyle,
- Brandon
